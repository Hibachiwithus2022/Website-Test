That intentionality carries over to dinner. A group that flew into Miami and drove three hours to a rented house on the southernmost point isn't going to be satisfied with an average meal. They want dinner to match the trip.

Private hibachi delivers that. A certified teppanyaki chef arrives at your rental property, sets up outside, and runs a live cooking performance for your group while you stay exactly where you already are. No reservations. No dress code. No driving after drinks. Just your group, your house, and a chef who makes the dinner the best part of the day.

## How Private Hibachi Works in Key West

Hibachi Connect sends a certified chef to your outdoor space. They arrive 30–45 minutes before your event start with a propane teppan grill, all fresh proteins and ingredients, plates, sauces, utensils, and everything else required to run a complete 90–120 minute live hibachi dinner. All you need is a flat outdoor surface — a backyard, a patio, a deck — with at least 10×10 feet of usable space and open-air ventilation. The chef handles setup, the full performance including the cooking show, service, and complete cleanup.

**What works in Key West:** Most Key West rental homes — particularly in Old Town, Midtown, and the Truman Annex area — have enclosed or semi-enclosed outdoor courtyards and patio spaces. Many have pools surrounded by patio decking, which works well for hibachi setup. Ground-floor or first-floor properties with dedicated outdoor space are the best setup.

**What doesn't work:** Fully enclosed indoor spaces, upper-floor condo units without private outdoor access, or narrow balconies without adequate floor space. If your rental is a condominium without private outdoor access, confirm the setup before booking.

## Planning a Key West Hibachi Dinner

**Book early.** Key West rental weeks fill months in advance, and your hibachi date will compete with everyone else who had the same idea for that Saturday in March or that last week of June. If your trip is booked, secure your hibachi dinner as soon as the date is confirmed — 4–6 weeks minimum for peak season dates.

**Confirm your outdoor space before booking.** Key West properties vary enormously. Send us a quick note about your rental's outdoor setup when you book — courtyard, pool deck, side patio — and we'll confirm the setup works before the chef travels.

**Group size:** Single station for 10–18 guests. Two stations for 20–30 guests. For Key West groups, the most common booking is 10–16 guests at a single station.
