The Georgia Golden Isles — primarily [St. Simons Island](/locations/georgia/st-simons-island) — are the South's most understated luxury coastal destination. Unlike the more crowded Florida beaches or the Carolinas' commercial shore towns, the Golden Isles have a quiet, natural character: live oaks draped in Spanish moss, low-key beach access, world-class golf at Sea Island, and a vacation rental and second-home market that draws Atlanta professionals and old-money Southern families.

Private hibachi connects exceptionally well with the Golden Isles market. Groups who come for a week at a large St. Simons rental or a Sea Island villa want one evening that gathers everyone for a real dinner — private hibachi provides that without competing with the island's restaurant scene.

## St. Simons Island

[St. Simons Island](/locations/georgia/st-simons-island) is the most accessible and most popular of the Golden Isles — connected to Brunswick by the F.J. Torras Causeway, with a town center, beaches, and a substantial vacation rental market. The properties here range from modest beach cottages to large estate homes in the upscale neighborhoods near Sea Island.

**Who books on St. Simons:**

*Atlanta families renting for a week* — The classic Golden Isles vacation: Atlanta families who drive down I-16 for a week in June or July and rent a large house near East Beach or in the Retreat neighborhood. A chef on the back deck or screened porch is the dinner event of the week.

*Multigenerational family reunions* — Extended families who pick St. Simons as the gathering point. The larger rental properties here accommodate 15 to 25 guests comfortably, and private hibachi solves the dinner logistics for the whole group.

*Sea Island guests* — Sea Island's resort villa and cottage program attracts a highly affluent clientele from Atlanta and beyond. Private hibachi at a Sea Island property is a natural format for the premium experience expectations this market has.

**St. Simons setup notes:** Many properties have screened porches or covered outdoor spaces — these work well for hibachi even during mild weather. The ocean breezes on St. Simons are gentler than the exposed OBX or Jersey Shore coastlines. Confirm flatness and outdoor dimensions (minimum 10x10 ft).

## Savannah Connection

[Savannah](/locations/georgia/savannah) is 70 miles north of St. Simons on I-95. Groups that rent in Savannah sometimes use the St. Simons corridor for a day trip; groups based at the Golden Isles may visit Savannah. Both markets work for private hibachi — see our [Savannah post](/blog/private-hibachi-savannah-georgia) for the city-specific guide.

## Georgia Coastal Season

The Golden Isles outdoor season runs year-round — coastal Georgia doesn't have a true off-season for outdoor events. Peak rental season is June through August. Spring (March through May) and fall (September through November) are excellent for outdoor events with more moderate temperatures.
