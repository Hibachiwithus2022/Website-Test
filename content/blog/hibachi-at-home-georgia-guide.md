Georgia's private dining landscape spans more range than most states realize. A [Atlanta](/locations/georgia/atlanta) backyard dinner party in Buckhead looks nothing like a [Savannah](/locations/georgia/savannah) vacation rental gathering in the Historic District, which looks nothing like a mountain cabin event in the North Georgia highlands. And yet a certified private hibachi chef works for all of it — the same live teppanyaki format, delivered to your outdoor space, wherever in Georgia that happens to be.

This guide covers how private hibachi works across Georgia's major markets, what to expect from a booking, and how to plan the right event for your property.

## What Private Hibachi at Home Actually Is

Hibachi Connect sends a certified teppanyaki chef to your outdoor space. They arrive with a propane teppan grill, all fresh proteins and vegetables, house-made sauces, plates, utensils, and everything needed to run a 90–120 minute live cooking show for your guests. You provide the outdoor space — at minimum a 10×10 foot flat surface — and seating. The chef handles everything else, including complete cleanup when dinner is done.

Pricing in Georgia is **$60 per adult** and **$30 per child** (12 & under), with a **$600 event minimum**. That covers the full chef experience, the live performance, and a complete meal with two proteins per person.

## Georgia's Major Markets

### Atlanta and the Metro Suburbs

[Atlanta](/locations/georgia/atlanta) is Georgia's dominant private event market and one of the largest in the Southeast. The metro spreads across a wide geographic footprint — Intown Atlanta, Buckhead, the near suburbs of [Marietta](/locations/georgia/marietta) and [Decatur](/locations/georgia/decatur), and the affluent North Atlanta corridor running through [Sandy Springs](/locations/georgia/sandy-springs), [Dunwoody](/locations/georgia/dunwoody), [Roswell](/locations/georgia/roswell), and [Alpharetta](/locations/georgia/alpharetta) up to [Johns Creek](/locations/georgia/johns-creek) and [Duluth](/locations/georgia/duluth).

The North Atlanta suburbs — particularly Alpharetta, Johns Creek, and Roswell — have the most consistently bookable property profiles: large subdivisions with generous rear yards, frequent outdoor entertaining, and a graduation/birthday/family celebration culture that drives consistent demand from April through October.

Intown Atlanta requires more setup confirmation. Smaller lots in Virginia-Highland, Morningside, and East Atlanta can work but need advance verification of outdoor square footage. Buckhead properties with estate-style outdoor spaces are ideal.

**Peak season in Atlanta:** Graduation weekends in May and June are the heaviest demand window. Book 5–6 weeks out for June Saturdays. Summer Saturdays July through September are the second peak. Fall outdoor entertaining (September–November) has become increasingly popular as temperatures become ideal for outdoor events.

### Savannah

[Savannah](/locations/georgia/savannah) is a vacation destination with a strong short-term rental culture. The Historic District's squares, the neighborhoods around Forsyth Park, and the vacation rentals along the South Carolina border on Tybee Island all draw group travelers who want an evening that doesn't require navigating Savannah's packed restaurant scene during peak season.

Private hibachi is particularly well-suited to Savannah vacation rental groups — bachelor parties, bachelorette weekends, birthday trips, and family reunions staying at a rented home in the Historic District or a coastal property near the water. The chef comes to your rental, the group stays in one place, and the evening runs at your pace.

**Setup consideration in Savannah:** Historic District properties often have beautiful but enclosed courtyard spaces. Confirm outdoor ventilation — teppanyaki cooking requires open-air clearance above the grill.

### Augusta

[Augusta](/locations/georgia/augusta) has two distinct seasons: Masters week (April) and everything else. For Masters week rental bookings, private hibachi is a natural fit — groups renting a home for the week in Augusta's residential neighborhoods often want one signature dinner event. Book extremely early for Masters week dates (December–January for April availability).

Outside Masters week, Augusta's residential market supports a standard birthday/graduation/family event calendar.

### Athens

[Athens](/locations/georgia/athens) has an active graduation and university event calendar tied to the University of Georgia. May graduation weekends are the peak window. Athens backyards and rental homes in Five Points, Normaltown, and the neighborhoods surrounding campus support single-station hibachi setups for 10–20 guests.

### Golden Isles and the Coast

[St. Simons Island](/locations/georgia/st-simons-island) and the Georgia Golden Isles attract the same vacation rental demographic as Florida's barrier islands — family weeks, milestone celebrations, and friend group trips staying at beachside or Intracoastal properties. Outdoor entertaining spaces on the Georgia coast are generally well-suited to hibachi: open-air screened porches, elevated decks, and pool patios with generous square footage.

## Outdoor Season in Georgia

Georgia's outdoor entertaining season runs April through October, with summer (June–August) being the most active window. Shoulder season — April–May and September–October — often has the most comfortable temperatures for outdoor events. December through February can work for covered outdoor spaces but needs weather confirmation for open-air events.
