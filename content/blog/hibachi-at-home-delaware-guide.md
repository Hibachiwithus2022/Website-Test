Delaware has two distinct private hibachi markets. The northern market — [Wilmington](/locations/delaware/wilmington), [Newark](/locations/delaware/newark), [Hockessin](/locations/delaware/hockessin), [Greenville](/locations/delaware/greenville) — is a affluent suburban corridor directly adjacent to the Philadelphia metro, drawing from the same financial services and pharmaceutical economy. The southern market — [Rehoboth Beach](/locations/delaware/rehoboth-beach), [Bethany Beach](/locations/delaware/bethany-beach), [Lewes](/locations/delaware/lewes), [Dewey Beach](/locations/delaware/dewey-beach) — is a coastal vacation rental market that draws Baltimore, DC, and Philadelphia groups for summer weekends.

Both markets support private hibachi well. Here's how it works across the state.

## What Private Hibachi at Home Means

A certified teppanyaki chef arrives at your property with a propane teppan grill, all fresh ingredients, plates, utensils, and sauces. They set up independently, run a 90 to 120 minute live cooking performance for your guests, and clean up completely when done. You provide a flat outdoor surface of at least 10x10 feet.

Pricing is **$60 per adult** and **$30 per child** (12 and under), with a **$600 event minimum**.

## Wilmington and the Northern Corridor

[Wilmington](/locations/delaware/wilmington) is Delaware's largest city and its corporate capital — a financial and legal hub known for its favorable corporate law environment. The neighborhoods north and west of the city proper form an affluent residential corridor that extends into [Greenville](/locations/delaware/greenville), [Hockessin](/locations/delaware/hockessin), and [Pike Creek](/locations/delaware/pike-creek).

**Greenville** is Delaware's most prestigious address — large estate properties, corporate executives, and old money families with significant outdoor entertaining spaces. Private hibachi here tends toward intimate dinner parties of 12 to 20 guests with premium upgrade preferences.

**Hockessin** is a quieter, family-focused community northwest of Wilmington with established residential lots and strong graduation event demand.

**[Newark](/locations/delaware/newark)** is home to the University of Delaware. Graduation season in May generates consistent family celebration demand, with families hosting 20 to 35 guests for post-ceremony dinners.

**[Claymont](/locations/delaware/claymont) and [New Castle](/locations/delaware/new-castle)** — the more densely developed areas closer to the Pennsylvania border — have varied residential profiles. Confirm outdoor square footage.

## Dover and Central Delaware

[Dover](/locations/delaware/dover) is Delaware's state capital and home to Delaware State University and Wesley College. Graduation season and family milestone events are the primary market. Dover's residential neighborhoods have suburban lot profiles with generally adequate outdoor setups.

Dover Air Force Base generates military community event demand — homecoming celebrations, promotion parties, and family milestone events.

## The Delaware Beaches: Rehoboth, Bethany, Dewes, and Lewes

The Delaware beach towns are a classic East Coast summer vacation rental market — drawing primarily from Baltimore, Washington DC, Philadelphia, and the broader Mid-Atlantic corridor. These groups rent houses sleeping 10 to 20 people for a week at a time, and private hibachi gives them one exceptional dinner night during the stay.

**[Rehoboth Beach](/locations/delaware/rehoboth-beach)** is the most popular and densely developed of the Delaware beaches. The rental properties range from modest beach cottages to large oceanfront homes — the larger properties, particularly on the north side of the canal and in the North Shores area, have better outdoor setups.

**[Lewes](/locations/delaware/lewes)** is the upscale alternative to Rehoboth — quieter, with larger properties, strong sailing and boating culture, and an older demographic. Private dinner party formats work well here.

**[Bethany Beach](/locations/delaware/bethany-beach), [Dewey Beach](/locations/delaware/dewey-beach), and [Fenwick Island](/locations/delaware/fenwick-island)** are smaller communities that draw specific audiences: Bethany is family-focused, Dewey is the party-crowd beach, and Fenwick Island is the southernmost and quietest.

**Delaware beach setup notes:** Ocean breezes are significant at beach properties — confirm wind protection for the cooking area. Upper-deck setups may require additional planning. Street-level or garden-level outdoor spaces with wind breaks work best.
