Arkansas has five major graduation markets — four university cities and one concentrated high school commencement corridor in Northwest Arkansas — and every one of them shares the same challenge: the best tables are gone weeks before the ceremony, and finding a dinner format that works for 20 or 30 people at a single table is nearly impossible at a local restaurant in May.

Private hibachi is how Arkansas graduation families have solved this problem. The chef comes to your home, your rental property, or your backyard — completely self-contained — and performs the full teppanyaki dinner for every guest at once. Grandparents from out of state, cousins who flew in, the graduate's closest friends, and everyone's parents all sit at the same grill for two uninterrupted hours.

This guide covers every major Arkansas graduation market: what to book, when to book, and what to expect.

## University of Arkansas — Fayetteville

[Fayetteville](/locations/arkansas/fayetteville) commencement is the single most competitive graduation booking window in Arkansas. The University of Arkansas typically holds spring commencement ceremonies across multiple days in May, drawing families from every corner of the state and from neighboring Missouri, Oklahoma, Kansas, and Texas.

Every Fayetteville restaurant knows these weeks. Reservations for groups of 12 or more disappear 6–8 weeks before commencement. The families who wait until after the graduation date is confirmed — typically in January or February — often find that every private dining option within reasonable distance is already claimed.

### Why Private Hibachi Works for UA Graduation

The [Fayetteville](/locations/arkansas/fayetteville) and [Springdale](/locations/arkansas/springdale) residential areas surrounding the UA campus have exactly the outdoor entertaining infrastructure private hibachi requires — backyard patios, covered outdoor spaces, and enough room for the complete self-contained grill setup. Many families host their UA graduation parties at homes in Johnson, Elkins, Farmington, or Prairie Grove — all within easy travel distance for the chef.

For families who live outside the Fayetteville area and are renting a property for graduation weekend: rental homes in Rogers, Bentonville, and Springdale regularly serve as graduation party venues for UA families coming in from out of state. The private hibachi chef travels to whatever property you're using — no adjustment needed.

**Booking window for UA graduation:** Confirm your date 5–7 weeks before commencement. For the specific Saturday or Sunday immediately after the ceremony, 6–8 weeks is safer.

## University of Central Arkansas — Conway

[Conway](/locations/arkansas/conway) is one of the most graduation-event-dense cities in Arkansas relative to its population — a university town with three institutions on the same academic calendar (UCA, Hendrix College, and Central Baptist College) that generates more concurrent graduation parties per square mile in May than almost anywhere in the state.

The Conway challenge is volume. When UCA commencement, Hendrix commencement, and Central Baptist commencement overlap in the same week — which they frequently do — every restaurant in Faulkner County with group seating capability is fully reserved. The families hosting graduation parties in Conway during this window either booked months in advance or they're improvising.

### The Conway Graduation Format

Private hibachi at a Conway home is the solution for a simple reason: it scales to any group size and requires no reservation. Your chef arrives at your Conway property before your guests do. A group of 14 from out of state who flew into Little Rock and drove to Conway? Handled. A group of 32 including grandparents from Fort Smith and cousins from Tulsa? Single grill for 25 and under, dual station option for larger groups.

The neighborhoods that host the most Conway graduation events — College Avenue corridor homes, subdivisions off Dave Ward Drive, and the newer developments in North Conway — all have the backyard and patio setups that work naturally for this format.

**Booking window for Conway graduation:** Confirm your date 4–5 weeks before commencement. The same weeks when UCA, Hendrix, and Central Baptist commencements overlap are the most competitive — book those specific dates 5–6 weeks out.

## Arkansas Tech University — Russellville

[Russellville](/locations/arkansas/russellville) hosts ATU graduation in May, drawing families from across the Arkansas River Valley and from communities throughout the state. The Russellville restaurant scene is smaller than Fayetteville or Conway, which makes the graduation table shortage more acute — there are simply fewer options to begin with.

ATU graduation parties in Russellville divide into two categories: families who live in Russellville or Pope County and are hosting at their own homes, and families who've traveled to Russellville for commencement and are using a rental property or a local family member's home as the party venue.

Both scenarios work identically with private hibachi. The chef arrives at the address you provide, confirms outdoor setup space, and performs the dinner. The chef travels to Russellville from across Central Arkansas — distance isn't a barrier.

One Russellville-specific angle: some ATU graduation families combine the graduation party with a Lake Dardanelle weekend trip, hosting the party at a cabin or vacation rental on the reservoir. This is a natural pairing — the graduation dinner becomes a Saturday-evening lake house experience, and the rest of the weekend is spent on the water.

**Booking window for ATU graduation:** Confirm your date 4–5 weeks before commencement.

## Arkansas State University — Jonesboro

[Jonesboro](/locations/arkansas/jonesboro) and Arkansas State commencement bring families to Northeast Arkansas from the Memphis suburbs, the Missouri Bootheel, and rural Arkansas communities from Craighead, Greene, Mississippi, and surrounding counties. For families traveling from Memphis or West Memphis, this graduation weekend is often the furthest they travel all year for a family event — which makes the quality of the celebration more important, not less.

The Jonesboro restaurant scene has expanded significantly as the city has grown, but graduation weekend still exhausts reservation availability. Private hibachi at a Jonesboro home — or at a property in Bay, Cash, Nettleton, or any surrounding community — handles the group that no restaurant table can.

The A-State graduation demographic skews toward larger family gatherings. Extended family structures in rural Northeast Arkansas mean graduation parties where three sets of grandparents and a dozen cousins all need to be at the same table — exactly the format private hibachi is designed for.

**Booking window for A-State graduation:** Confirm your date 4–6 weeks before commencement. The specific Saturday and Sunday after the ceremony fill fastest.

## Northwest Arkansas High School Commencement Market

The [Bentonville](/locations/arkansas/bentonville), [Rogers](/locations/arkansas/rogers), [Fayetteville](/locations/arkansas/fayetteville), and [Springdale](/locations/arkansas/springdale) high school graduation calendar in late May and early June is a separate booking window from the university calendar — and in some ways more competitive, because the families hosting these parties tend to be NWA executive and professional households who expect quality.

### Key NWA High School Commencement Dates

The major NWA high school graduations typically fall in the last two weeks of May through the first week of June:
- **Bentonville West and Bentonville East** — back-to-back May/June ceremonies drawing from the Walmart executive residential community
- **Rogers Heritage and Rogers High** — ceremonies that draw from the Pinnacle Hills residential corridor
- **Fayetteville High** — largest Fayetteville graduation, drawing families from across the city
- **Springdale Har-Ber and Springdale High** — two separate ceremonies for Springdale's large population
- **Bella Vista** (Rogers Heritage feeder) — students from Bella Vista attending Rogers Heritage graduate at Rogers Heritage commencement

These graduation weekends collectively represent the most concentrated private hibachi demand window in Arkansas. NWA executive families hosting graduation parties for 20–30 guests at their Bentonville or Rogers homes compete for the same chef availability.

**Booking window for NWA high school graduations:** The most in-demand dates — any Saturday or Sunday during the NWA graduation corridor — should be confirmed 6–8 weeks ahead. Book as soon as the graduation date and venue are confirmed.

## Practical Guidance for Any Arkansas Graduation Party

**Group size:** The format works from 10 to 60+ guests. For groups under 25, a single teppan station handles everyone simultaneously. For groups of 25–35, dual station setups are available — two chefs, two grills, full performance for the larger group.

**Menu:** Every guest orders individually from the menu. The standard menu includes chicken, steak, shrimp, and salmon, plus fried rice, grilled vegetables, miso soup, yum yum sauce, ginger sauce, plates, and chopsticks. Premium upgrades — filet mignon, lobster tail, Chilean sea bass, wagyu — can be added for individual guests or for the entire group.

**Setup requirements:** A 12×12 outdoor space is all that's needed. Most Arkansas backyard patios, covered outdoor areas, and deck spaces handle this without any modification. The grill is propane-powered — no gas hookup required, no kitchen access needed.

**Duration:** 90–120 minutes for the full dinner service. Plan your graduation party schedule around this — guests arrive, chef performs the dinner, and the evening naturally continues after dinner without any logistics pressure.

**Cleanup:** Complete. Everything the chef brought is packed out. Your property is exactly as it was.
