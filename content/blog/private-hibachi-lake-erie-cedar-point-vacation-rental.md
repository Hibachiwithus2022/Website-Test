## What the Setup Looks Like at a Lake Erie Vacation Rental

Most Lake Erie vacation rental hosts have never had a private chef before and don't know exactly what to expect. Here's exactly how it works:

**Before the event:** When you book, tell us the property address. We'll confirm the surface type and space. Deck, patio, driveway, grass — all work. A flat 10×10 area is all we need.

**Day of arrival:** Your chef arrives 30–45 minutes before the designated dinner time. They bring the full grill, all ingredients, all equipment, and set up independently. You don't need to move anything, prep anything, or clear the deck — the chef handles it.

**During the event:** The full teppanyaki performance runs 90–120 minutes. Fire tricks, flying shrimp, knife skills, and every course cooked live in front of your group. For groups over 25, we bring two chefs and two stations so everyone eats at the same time.

**After the event:** Complete cleanup. We pack out everything we brought, wipe down the cooking area, and leave the property in the condition we found it. Your vacation rental checkout is unaffected.

## Frequently Asked Questions for Lake Erie Events

**Do you need a gas hookup at the vacation rental?**
No. Our teppan grill is fully self-contained with a propane system. We bring our own fuel. Lake houses, island rentals, and properties without outdoor gas lines are all fine.

**What surface can the chef cook on?**
Deck, concrete patio, driveway, grass, or packed gravel all work. We bring our own grill stand. The only requirement is a flat 10×10 area. Wood decks are fine — we use a heat shield beneath the grill.

**What if it rains?**
We can move to a garage, a large covered porch, or an indoor space with good ventilation. When you book, we'll discuss your backup option so you're never left without a plan.

**Do you travel to the islands (Put-in-Bay, Kelleys Island)?**
Yes — travel to the islands is factored into your quote at booking. We coordinate ferry transport and logistics. Just tell us the island property address when you inquire.

**How much space do we need for a large group?**
For groups over 25 we bring two chefs and two stations, which requires approximately 10×20 feet of outdoor clearance. If your rental deck is narrower, we work around the property layout — just let us know the setup when booking.
