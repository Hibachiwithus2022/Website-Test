The North Atlanta suburbs form one of the Southeast's most active private hibachi markets. The corridor from [Sandy Springs](/locations/georgia/sandy-springs) north through [Roswell](/locations/georgia/roswell), [Alpharetta](/locations/georgia/alpharetta), [Johns Creek](/locations/georgia/johns-creek), and [Dunwoody](/locations/georgia/dunwoody) is home to a dense concentration of tech industry employees (Microsoft, NCR, Fiserv, Salesforce, Cox Enterprises), biomedical and healthcare professionals, and one of the largest South Asian communities in the South. The result is a consistent, high-volume private event market that spans graduation parties, corporate team events, birthday milestones, and family celebrations.

## Alpharetta

[Alpharetta](/locations/georgia/alpharetta) is Georgia's tech capital — a planned tech corridor north of Atlanta where Microsoft, NCR, Fiserv, ADP, Lam Research, and dozens of other major tech companies have offices. The residential communities here (Windward, Webb Bridge, Milton Road corridor) have large newer homes with covered patios, outdoor kitchens, and pool decks that make ideal private event setups.

**Who books in Alpharetta:** Tech industry households hosting corporate team events at executive homes. South Asian community graduation dinners for high school and college completions. Milestone birthday parties for 25 to 40 guests.

## Johns Creek

[Johns Creek](/locations/georgia/johns-creek) has consistently ranked as one of Georgia's safest and highest-income cities. The South Asian community here — particularly the Indian-American community — is among the most concentrated in the Southeast, and graduation event demand from Johns Creek Northview and other top-performing high schools is extremely high.

**Johns Creek setup:** The residential properties here are almost universally large newer homes with significant outdoor spaces — pool decks, covered patios, and rear yards that handle groups of 25 to 50 guests without difficulty.

## Roswell and Dunwoody

[Roswell](/locations/georgia/roswell) and [Dunwoody](/locations/georgia/dunwoody) are established North Atlanta suburbs with a mix of historic residential areas and newer development. Roswell's Historic District has older homes with smaller lots; the newer development areas around the city have much better outdoor setups. Dunwoody's residential areas (Dunwoody Club Forest, Georgetown, Dunwoody North) have consistently good suburban lot profiles.

## Sandy Springs and Marietta

[Sandy Springs](/locations/georgia/sandy-springs) is the first North Fulton suburb immediately north of Atlanta's city limits. It sits at the intersection of Atlanta's corporate headquarters belt (UPS, Cox, Intercontinental Exchange, Equifax all have offices nearby) and an affluent residential corridor along the Chattahoochee River. The residential areas in Sandy Springs have varied lot sizes — confirm outdoor square footage.

[Marietta](/locations/georgia/marietta) is Cobb County's historic center and home to Kennesaw State University. The East Cobb neighborhoods north and east of Marietta — nationally recognized for their public schools — have excellent residential properties with generous suburban lots. Graduation season at Lassiter, Pope, and Walton High Schools and at KSU generates strong demand in May and June.
