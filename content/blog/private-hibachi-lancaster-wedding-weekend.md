## Why Hibachi Works for a Lancaster Wedding Rehearsal Dinner

The rehearsal dinner has specific demands that most formats handle poorly.

**Large, varied guest lists.** Forty to sixty people is standard. That group includes both families meeting each other for the first time, friends from different parts of the couple's life, and grandparents alongside college roommates. The hibachi format seats everyone together — no private dining room split across three tables, no buffet line that disperses the group. One chef, one table, one shared experience.

**Out-of-town guests who came for an experience.** When guests have traveled from across the country for a Lancaster wedding weekend, they want the rehearsal dinner to feel like an event, not a logistics solution. Private hibachi delivers that — the live teppanyaki performance is something most guests have never seen in a private setting, and it creates a first-night energy that sets up the weekend beautifully.

**Farm venue and property versatility.** Lancaster's wedding weekend properties — farm venues, estate vacation rentals, agritourism destinations — all have outdoor space that works for a hibachi setup. Our chef brings a self-contained propane grill with no venue infrastructure requirements. No commercial kitchen access needed. No gas hookup. We set up anywhere.

**Complete cleanup.** Farm venue rental agreements typically require the property to be restored after each event. Our chef handles all cleanup completely — the cooking area is left exactly as we found it.

## What the Evening Looks Like

The private hibachi rehearsal dinner format is consistent regardless of property type.

**Chef arrives 30–45 minutes early** to set up the teppan grill, arrange the cooking station, and prep ingredients. No advance venue coordination required from the couple or wedding planner.

**The performance begins** when your guests are seated. Knife skills. Volcano tricks. Fire shows. The chef cooks every protein and side dish fresh in front of the group — steak, chicken, shrimp, salmon, lobster tail, rice, noodles, vegetables, house-made sauces. The performance creates a shared experience that immediately establishes the weekend's celebratory tone.

**Everyone eats together.** No course delays. No kitchen timing coordination. No server sequences. The chef serves each plate as it comes off the grill — the group eats simultaneously, which creates exactly the kind of collective energy a rehearsal dinner is designed to build.

**Complete cleanup.** When the last plate is cleared and the performance is complete, we break down the grill, remove all equipment, and clean the cooking surface. Your farm venue or rental property is left in the condition required by your rental agreement.
