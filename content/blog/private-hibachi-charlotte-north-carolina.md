[Charlotte](/locations/north-carolina/charlotte) has built more new suburban development in the last 20 years than almost any city in the South — and all that development means backyards. Large, well-designed, outdoor-entertaining-ready backyards in [Huntersville](/locations/north-carolina/huntersville), [Mooresville](/locations/north-carolina/mooresville), [Matthews](/locations/north-carolina/matthews), [Concord](/locations/north-carolina/concord), and [Lake Norman](/locations/north-carolina/lake-norman). These are neighborhoods built by people who moved from the Northeast and Midwest with high expectations for how outdoor space should work — and they use it.

Private hibachi fits this market well. A certified teppanyaki chef comes to your property, sets up on the patio, and runs a live cooking performance for your guests. No restaurant logistics. No compromise on the food. The dinner becomes the event.

## The Charlotte Suburbs — Where Hibachi Works Best

**[Huntersville](/locations/north-carolina/huntersville)** is the northern suburb with the strongest backyard profile for private events. The Birkdale Village corridor and the neighborhoods along NC-73 have newer construction with open rear yards, patio setups adjacent to pools, and the kind of space that makes dual-station hibachi for 30+ guests entirely workable.

**[Lake Norman](/locations/north-carolina/lake-norman)** is a destination within the Charlotte metro. Waterfront homes with elevated decks overlooking the lake, boat docks and large rear properties, and an event culture tied to the region's strong second-home and primary residence market. A lake house hibachi dinner — sunset behind the water, chef running a live station on the deck — is one of the best versions of this format in North Carolina.

**[Mooresville](/locations/north-carolina/mooresville)** runs alongside Lake Norman with similar residential profiles. Race shop country (NASCAR has strong roots here) with a mix of estate and suburban lots. The properties along the lake and in the newer developments off Brawley School Road have excellent outdoor setups.

**[Matthews](/locations/north-carolina/matthews) and [Concord](/locations/north-carolina/concord)** anchor the eastern and northeastern Charlotte metro. Matthews has an established residential feel — older neighborhoods with mature yards and consistent graduation/birthday demand. Concord sits adjacent to Charlotte Motor Speedway and has strong suburban growth with large newer-construction lots.

**South Charlotte (Ballantyne, Pineville, Tega Cay)** rounds out the metro. Ballantyne's planned community lots are reliable for hibachi setup. Tega Cay, South Carolina — just across the state line from Charlotte — also falls in our Charlotte service area.

## Who Books in the Charlotte Metro

**Graduation parties.** Charlotte's rapid population growth means a lot of young families with high school and college-age kids. May and June graduation Saturdays in Huntersville and Matthews are some of the highest-demand dates in the Carolinas. Book 5–6 weeks out.

**Corporate entertaining.** Charlotte is a major banking and finance hub — Bank of America and Wells Fargo are both headquartered here, along with Lowe's and a substantial financial services sector. Senior executives entertaining clients or teams at home use private hibachi as an alternative to restaurant reservations.

**Birthday parties and milestone dinners.** The 40th through 60th birthday backyard party is a Charlotte staple. Hibachi adds the live performance element that keeps the evening energized.

**Lake Norman destination events.** Groups that rent a Lake Norman property for a weekend — from Charlotte, from elsewhere in the Carolinas, from out of state — frequently add a private hibachi dinner as the anchor evening event.

## Season and Booking

Charlotte's outdoor season runs April through October. Spring (April–May) and fall (September–November) are the most comfortable for outdoor events. Summer is busy but manageable — evening temperatures in June and July are warm but not oppressive.
