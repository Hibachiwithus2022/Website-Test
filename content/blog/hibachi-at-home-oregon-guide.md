Oregon has three distinct private hibachi markets. The Portland metro — [Portland](/locations/oregon/portland), [Beaverton](/locations/oregon/beaverton), [Hillsboro](/locations/oregon/hillsboro), [Lake Oswego](/locations/oregon/lake-oswego) — is an urban and suburban market with a strong outdoor culture and a food-literate population that cares about what goes on the plate. [Bend](/locations/oregon/bend) is a mountain tourism destination that draws vacation rental groups year-round. And the [Oregon Coast](/locations/oregon/cannon-beach) — Cannon Beach, Lincoln City — is classic vacation rental territory where groups want one exceptional dinner during the week.

All three support private hibachi. Here's the full picture.

## What Private Hibachi at Home Means

A certified teppanyaki chef arrives at your outdoor space with everything needed to run a complete live hibachi dinner: propane teppan grill, all fresh ingredients, plates, utensils, sauces. They set up, perform and cook for 90–120 minutes, and clean up completely at the end. You provide a flat outdoor surface of at least 10×10 feet.

Pricing is **$60 per adult** and **$30 per child** (12 & under), with a **$600 event minimum**.

## Portland and the Metro Suburbs

[Portland](/locations/oregon/portland) has a food culture that's disproportionately serious for a city of its size. Portland residents who care about what they eat — and many do — want a private hibachi experience that matches their standards. The city's outdoor culture, strong backyard entertaining ethic, and mix of tech workers (Intel, Nike, adidas, Precision Castparts) with high discretionary income makes the metro one of the more active hibachi markets in the Pacific Northwest.

**[Beaverton](/locations/oregon/beaverton) and [Hillsboro](/locations/oregon/hillsboro)** are where the tech industry concentrates — Intel's largest campus is in Hillsboro, Nike's global headquarters is in Beaverton. These are suburban cities with strong South Asian and East Asian communities that drive consistent graduation and family event demand. The residential areas around Intel's campuses have newer construction with better outdoor setups than Portland proper.

**[Lake Oswego](/locations/oregon/lake-oswego)** is Portland's most affluent suburb — a lakeside residential community with large properties, strong outdoor entertaining culture, and event expectations that match. Lake Oswego bookings tend toward intimate dinner parties of 12–20 guests with premium upgrade preferences.

**Portland proper setup notes:** Portland's older neighborhoods (Sellwood, Division, Alberta Arts District, Hawthorne) have smaller urban lots with variable outdoor space. Confirm square footage carefully. The outer east side, Southwest Portland, and the newer developments in North Portland have more generous yards.

## Bend

[Bend](/locations/oregon/bend) is one of Oregon's most dynamic markets — a mountain outdoor recreation city that draws visitors year-round for skiing, hiking, climbing, and cycling, with a vacation rental stock that fills up most weekends. Groups that come to Bend for a weekend often want one evening that gathers everyone for a real dinner rather than fighting the downtown restaurant scene.

Private hibachi works extremely well for Bend vacation rental groups. The properties here frequently have outdoor deck or patio spaces with mountain or high desert views. A chef on the deck, dinner running as the sun drops behind the Cascades — it's a genuinely compelling evening.

**Bend outdoor season:** Summer (June–September) is prime. Fall and spring work well. Winter Bend bookings need a covered outdoor space given the elevation.

## Oregon Coast: Cannon Beach and Lincoln City

[Cannon Beach](/locations/oregon/cannon-beach) is Oregon's most iconic beach destination — Haystack Rock, dramatic cliffs, and vacation rentals that draw groups from Portland and beyond for weekend getaways. The properties here, particularly the larger vacation homes that sleep 10–20 guests, have outdoor deck setups that work for hibachi when the weather cooperates.

[Lincoln City](/locations/oregon/lincoln-city) is the central coast's most popular destination. Vacation rental groups who come for the Pacific views and the outlet stores often want one evening that feels like an event — private hibachi gives them that.

**Oregon Coast weather note:** The coast is famously unpredictable. Book flexibly and have an indoor backup plan for coast bookings. Covered decks and enclosed (but ventilated) outdoor porches work for hibachi during mild conditions.
