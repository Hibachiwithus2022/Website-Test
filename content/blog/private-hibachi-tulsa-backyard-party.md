## Why Tulsa Backyards Work for Private Hibachi

The private hibachi format has one fundamental requirement: outdoor space that accommodates a six-foot teppan grill station, a chef who needs about three feet of working space, and your guest seating within conversational distance of the grill. Most Tulsa metro growth corridor properties clear this threshold easily.

**South Tulsa new construction:** Bixby, Jenks, and south Broken Arrow have seen significant new construction over the past decade, and these homes almost universally include the kind of outdoor entertaining setup that makes private hibachi seamless: covered patios of 400–600 square feet, pergolas, outdoor kitchens with adjacent flat space, and pools with surrounding deck area. The chef can set up, perform, and clean up without any rearrangement of your permanent outdoor furniture.

**Older Broken Arrow and Owasso neighborhoods:** Rose District-area Broken Arrow homes and established Owasso neighborhoods may have more traditional backyard layouts — which work perfectly well. An open lawn area adjacent to a patio, or a back deck that seats 15 comfortably, is all that's needed. The self-contained propane teppan grill doesn't require any gas hookup or permanent outdoor utility.

**Pool decks:** Several Bixby and Jenks pool deck layouts are particularly well-suited because the pool deck surrounds the grill on multiple sides, giving guests natural seating along the perimeter while the chef works. This creates a theater-in-the-round dynamic for the performance.

## The Chef Arrival and Setup Process

**Arrival:** The chef arrives 20–30 minutes before the scheduled start time. For a backyard event in Bixby or Jenks, this means the chef drives directly to your address, arrives at the back gate or side yard access, and begins unloading.

**Equipment:** The chef brings the full self-contained setup — the propane teppan grill on its steel frame, all proteins, rice, vegetables, sauces, condiments, plates, chopsticks, and every serving item. Nothing needs to come from your kitchen.

**Setup time:** 15–20 minutes to position the grill, test propane, set up the ingredient prep station, and arrange the serving area. During this window, your guests can be gathering, drinking, or watching the setup.

**Performance:** 90–120 minutes for a standard group of 12–20. The chef performs the full teppanyaki sequence — fire starter, vegetable fried rice, each protein in sequence, the volcano, flying shrimp for any guests who want to try it — then plates and serves each order individually.

**Cleanup:** The chef handles all cleanup at the grill station. The propane unit, all equipment, and all food waste depart with the chef. Your backyard is exactly as it was before the event.

## Outdoor Setup Requirements

The private hibachi format requires:

- **Flat, stable surface** for the grill (concrete, wood deck, stone patio, or packed lawn)
- **Minimum 8×10 ft** of open space for the grill station plus chef working area
- **Guest seating** within 10–15 feet of the grill (folding tables, patio furniture, or any configuration that lets guests see the performance)
- **No overhead obstruction** within 6 feet above the grill surface (low pergolas, deck covers, and pergola covers at standard 8+ ft height are fine)
- **Gate or pathway access** from the street or driveway for equipment load-in
