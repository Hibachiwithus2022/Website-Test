Louisiana's graduation season runs from late April through mid-May and generates more concentrated restaurant reservation pressure than any other time of year in the state. LSU commencement alone draws tens of thousands of family members into Baton Rouge over three or four days. UL Lafayette, Tulane, Loyola, and Southeastern Louisiana all hold their ceremonies in the same six-week window. And across the state, the extended family structures that define Louisiana celebration culture mean a graduation party of 20 or 25 people is not unusual — it's the norm.

Private hibachi is how Louisiana graduation families solve the problem that every restaurant in May cannot. A chef arrives at your home or rental property, sets up with the complete self-contained grill, and performs the full teppanyaki dinner for your entire group at once. The graduate, their grandparents, their college friends, their siblings, their cousins, and both sets of parents all eat together for 90 minutes without a reservation battle or a split-table compromise.

This guide covers every major Louisiana graduation market.

## LSU Baton Rouge — The State's Largest Graduation Event

Louisiana State University commencement is the single largest graduation event in Louisiana. Held across multiple days in mid-May, LSU draws families from every Louisiana parish and from neighboring Texas, Mississippi, Arkansas, and Alabama. The timing — overlapping with the peak spring entertaining season — means restaurant reservation capacity across the entire metro is exhausted during commencement week.

[Baton Rouge](/locations/louisiana/baton-rouge) families hosting graduation parties at their Bocage or Beaumont subdivision homes, [Gonzales](/locations/louisiana/gonzales) and [Prairieville](/locations/louisiana/prairieville) families in Ascension Parish, and out-of-state families renting houses in the metro for the weekend all face the same situation: a group of 15 or 20 guests who need a dinner that matches the occasion, and no restaurant table that can do it on the right day.

Private hibachi at your Baton Rouge-area home solves this cleanly. The chef travels from across the metro to your address, confirms outdoor setup space, and performs the dinner for your group. The geographic range is wide — Zachary, Central, Denham Springs, and all of East Baton Rouge Parish are within normal travel distance.

**Booking window for LSU graduation:** The specific Saturday and Sunday after each commencement ceremony are the most competitive dates. Book these 6–8 weeks before commencement. For other May weekend dates in the LSU corridor, 4–6 weeks ahead is sufficient.

## UL Lafayette — The Acadiana Graduation Market

University of Louisiana at Lafayette commencement is the anchor event for the Acadiana graduation season. [Lafayette](/locations/louisiana/lafayette) families, [Youngsville](/locations/louisiana/youngsville) and [Broussard](/locations/louisiana/broussard) households in the Lafayette corridor, and families driving in from New Iberia, Breaux Bridge, and Opelousas all converge on Lafayette in May for UL commencement.

Lafayette's restaurant scene has grown significantly but still faces the same graduation-weekend pressure as every other Louisiana university city — the tables that exist are booked, and groups of 18 or 20 have nowhere to go. Private hibachi at a Lafayette or Youngsville home changes this completely.

One Acadiana-specific angle: the Cajun family gathering scale. A UL Lafayette graduation party that involves the graduate's family from Church Point, cousins from Breaux Bridge, and grandparents from New Iberia routinely involves 25 or 30 guests. This is exactly the group size the private hibachi format is designed for — every guest at the same grill, every protein order individual, the chef managing all of it simultaneously.

**Booking window for UL graduation:** Confirm your date 4–5 weeks before commencement. Peak UL graduation weekend dates (the Saturday and Sunday after the ceremony) should be confirmed 5–6 weeks ahead.

## Tulane and Loyola — New Orleans Commencement Season

Tulane University and Loyola University New Orleans both hold spring commencement in mid-May. These ceremonies draw families from across the country — New York, Chicago, California, and the upper Midwest to Tulane specifically, which has a nationally distributed alumni base. For families traveling to [New Orleans](/locations/louisiana/new-orleans) for graduation weekend, the private hibachi format at a vacation rental is often the preferred dinner option.

[Metairie](/locations/louisiana/metairie) and [Kenner](/locations/louisiana/kenner) families hosting Tulane or Loyola graduation dinners at their Jefferson Parish homes represent the residential version of this market — local families whose graduate's college friends and extended family from out of state fill the backyard for a graduation dinner that feels appropriately celebratory.

The New Orleans graduation market coincides with the tail end of Jazz Fest, which means rental property availability and restaurant reservation capacity are both under pressure simultaneously. This makes the "chef comes to your property" format even more valuable than usual.

**Booking window for Tulane/Loyola graduation:** Book 5–7 weeks before commencement. For New Orleans vacation rental events during graduation week, 6–8 weeks ahead is safer given the simultaneous Jazz Fest demand.

## Southeastern Louisiana University — Hammond and Tangipahoa Parish

Southeastern Louisiana University's spring commencement brings families to [Hammond](/locations/louisiana/hammond) from across Tangipahoa Parish and the I-12 corridor. For families driving in from New Orleans or Baton Rouge — 45 minutes in either direction — Hammond is familiar territory but an unfamiliar restaurant market: the local dining scene is sized for a college town, not for the graduation weekend surge.

Private hibachi at a Hammond home handles this directly. The chef arrives at your Tangipahoa Parish address, confirms outdoor setup, and performs the dinner for your group while you stay at the property rather than navigating the limited local restaurant options.

One Hammond-specific pattern: SLU graduation families sometimes combine the celebration with a drive-back party at a Baton Rouge or New Orleans property. If your graduation dinner is at a home elsewhere on the I-12 corridor, the same chef availability applies — we serve the full corridor between New Orleans and Baton Rouge.

**Booking window for SLU graduation:** Confirm 4–5 weeks before commencement.

## Other Louisiana University Graduation Markets

**Grambling State University and Louisiana Tech University** (both in Ruston, approximately 30 miles apart) serve the North-Central Louisiana graduation market. Ruston and Lincoln Parish families, families from Shreveport and [Bossier City](/locations/louisiana/bossier-city), and extended families from across North Louisiana gather for commencement in this corridor.

**McNeese State University** in Lake Charles anchors the Southwest Louisiana graduation market. [Lake Charles](/locations/louisiana/lake-charles) families hosting graduation parties at their homes use the private hibachi format the same way families do everywhere — chef at the house, full group at the same table, no restaurant reservation required.

**Centenary College** in Shreveport adds a smaller but consistent graduation demand to the [Shreveport](/locations/louisiana/shreveport) market. Centenary's smaller size means commencement gatherings are more intimate — typically 12 to 18 guests — which is well within the single-station hibachi format.

**Louisiana High School Graduation Season** — The major Louisiana metro areas have high school commencement corridors that add a separate booking window in late May and early June:
- **Greater Baton Rouge corridor:** Zachary, Central, Catholic High, University Lab — late May
- **Greater New Orleans corridor:** John Curtis, Brother Martin, Jesuit, and the Jefferson Parish public schools — late May through early June
- **Northshore corridor:** Mandeville High, Covington High, St. Paul's, Fontainebleau — late May

[Mandeville](/locations/louisiana/mandeville) and [Covington](/locations/louisiana/covington) Northshore families hosting high school graduation parties face particular demand pressure: the Northshore has a professional demographic that expects premium-quality celebration formats, and the restaurant scene in St. Tammany Parish is much smaller than the demand. Private hibachi at a Northshore home fills this gap well.

**Booking window for high school graduation:** Louisiana high school graduations run late May through early June. The peak weekend dates in the metro corridors — any Saturday or Sunday during the graduation calendar — should be confirmed 5–7 weeks ahead.

## Practical Guidance for Any Louisiana Graduation Party

**Group sizing:** Most Louisiana graduation parties run 14 to 28 guests. Single-station setup handles up to 25 guests simultaneously. For groups of 26–40, a dual-station option (two chefs, two grills) serves everyone at once with coordinated performance.

**Menu for graduation events:** Every guest orders individually. The chef handles multiple protein selections at the same grill — chicken, steak, shrimp, and salmon in any combination. Premium upgrades (filet mignon, lobster tail, wagyu) are available for the graduate or for the full group.

**Setup requirements:** A 12×12 outdoor space — patio, covered deck, or backyard area. Most Louisiana residential properties have adequate outdoor space without any modification.

**Timing:** The full dinner service runs 90–120 minutes. Plan your graduation evening around this — guests arrive, the chef performs the dinner, and the celebration continues naturally after dinner without logistics pressure.

**Cleanup:** Complete. Every item the chef brought is packed out. Your property is unchanged.
