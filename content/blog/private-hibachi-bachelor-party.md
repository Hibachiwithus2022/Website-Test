The bachelor party dinner question is always the same: where do you get a table for 15 guys that actually seats everyone together, doesn't feel like a chain restaurant, and gives the group something to watch during dinner?

Private hibachi solves all three. A certified teppanyaki chef comes to your backyard, vacation rental, or event space, sets up a propane teppan grill, and runs 90–120 minutes of live performance — fire tricks, knife work, flying shrimp, the works — while cooking every protein to order for the full group.

---

## Why It Works for Bachelor Parties

**One table, however large.** No splitting the group across reservations, no half the guys at one end of the bar and half at another. The chef cooks for everyone at once from a single setup.

**The performance is the entertainment.** Bachelor parties need something to watch. The hibachi performance — the fire volcano, the onion steam trick, the flying shrimp, the precision knife work — runs the full dinner. You're watching a show, not staring at menus.

**It works wherever the group is staying.** Backyard, vacation rental deck, lake house patio, beach house terrace, Airbnb with outdoor space. The chef is fully self-contained — propane grill, all proteins, all equipment, full cleanup. No venue coordination required.

**You control the food.** The chef cooks your group's order. Premium proteins across the whole table. Mix and match per person. Completely different from negotiating a prix-fixe group menu at a restaurant.

## Group Sizes

| Group | Setup | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| 8–20 | One chef station | Standard bachelor party format |
| 20–35 | One chef, service timing adjusted | Everyone eats; performance runs slightly longer |
| 35+ | Two stations | Dual setup; everyone eats simultaneously |

Most bachelor party groups run 12–20. If your count is above 25, note it at booking — the chef plans service timing accordingly. For groups of 35+, dual stations are strongly recommended.

## The Menu

**Standard (included with every event):**
- Hibachi chicken, steak, and shrimp
- Salmon on request
- Hibachi fried rice
- Lo mein noodles
- Vegetables (zucchini, mushrooms, onions)
- Miso soup
- Garden salad
- Ginger, yum yum, and teriyaki sauces

**Premium upgrades (per guest — mix and match):**
- Filet mignon
- Lobster tail
- Chilean sea bass
- King shrimp
- Wagyu beef

The most common bachelor party upgrade pattern: filet mignon and lobster tail for the groom, premium proteins across the table for the rest. Some groups go full wagyu for everyone. It's a bachelor party — upgrade.

## What You Need at Your Location

- **Space:** Approximately 10×10 feet of flat outdoor clearance (deck, patio, backyard lawn, terrace)
- **Access:** Vehicle access to the property for setup
- **That's it.** The chef brings everything else.

For vacation rentals: confirm with the host that outdoor open-flame cooking is permitted. It's allowed at most properties. A quick message to the host takes 30 seconds — do it before booking the chef.

## Booking Timeline

| Occasion | Lead Time |
|----------|-----------|
| Backyard bachelor party, off-peak | 1–2 weeks |
| City rental weekend, peak season | 3–5 weeks |
| Mountain or lake destination weekend | 4–8 weeks |
| Major event weekend (race, festival) | Book immediately |

The safest rule: book the chef the same week you confirm the venue or rental. The bachelor party date is fixed and the group is committed — the only variable is whether the chef date is still open.
