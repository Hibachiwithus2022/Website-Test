New Hampshire's private hibachi market is centered on two cities: [Manchester](/locations/new-hampshire/manchester), the state's largest city and economic hub, and [Concord](/locations/new-hampshire/concord), the state capital. Both sit within the southern New Hampshire corridor that benefits from Boston's economic gravity while maintaining a distinctly New England residential character — large lots, genuine backyards, and an outdoor season that runs strong from May through September.

## Manchester

[Manchester](/locations/new-hampshire/manchester) is New Hampshire's largest city and commercial center. Its residential neighborhoods spread across a mix of older in-city housing stock and suburban development that extends into Bedford, Goffstown, and the surrounding towns.

**Bedford** (adjacent to Manchester) is the metro's most affluent residential address — newer construction, large lots, and an outdoor entertaining culture that matches. Bedford bookings typically run larger graduation parties and milestone family dinners.

**Hooksett and south Manchester corridors** have strong family event demand — graduation parties and milestone birthday celebrations are the core market.

**What drives Manchester bookings:** Southern New Hampshire University and Saint Anselm College graduation seasons in May generate family celebration events. The tech and manufacturing sector along the Route 101 and I-293 corridors produces corporate team event demand.

## Concord

[Concord](/locations/new-hampshire/concord) is New Hampshire's state capital — a smaller, government-and-services city with a strong community character. The residential areas around Concord have suburban and semi-rural lot profiles with ample outdoor space. University of New Hampshire (40 minutes south in Durham) draws families back through the Concord area during graduation season.

## New Hampshire Outdoor Season

The outdoor entertaining season runs May through September. June through August are the peak months. Fall evenings in New Hampshire cool quickly — plan outdoor events to end before 9 PM in September and October. Book 3 to 4 weeks out for peak summer and graduation season dates.
