Cape Cod runs on vacation rentals — beach houses in [Chatham](/locations/massachusetts/chatham), family compounds in [Falmouth](/locations/massachusetts/falmouth), and everything in between anchored by [Hyannis](/locations/massachusetts/hyannis)'s ferry terminals and Main Street. Almost every trip out here shares the same shape: a group of family or friends, a rental house, and a few days of beach time that end with everyone back at the house, sunburnt and hungry.

A private hibachi chef has become one of the most requested additions to exactly that kind of trip — because after a day on Nantucket Sound, nobody wants to load a group of twelve into cars and find a restaurant that can seat everyone on a Saturday night in July.

## Why Vacation Rental Dinners Are the Default on the Cape

Hyannis, Chatham, and Falmouth are almost entirely vacation-rental economies — houses built for groups, with the kind of deck or backyard space that makes an outdoor group dinner easy to host. Our chefs arrive fully self-contained: a propane teppan grill that needs no gas hookup, all ingredients, and everything required to run the full show on your rental's patio or backyard. There's nothing to coordinate with the property beyond a flat outdoor space of at least 10×10 feet.

For a [Chatham](/locations/massachusetts/chatham) group coming off a day at Lighthouse Beach, or a [Hyannis](/locations/massachusetts/hyannis) family back from the ferry to Nantucket, that means the best meal of the trip happens without anyone having to leave the house.

## Family Reunions Built Around a Cape Cod Trip

Multi-generational family reunions are the single most common occasion we see on Cape Cod — grandparents, parents, and grandkids all converging on a rental house near [Falmouth](/locations/massachusetts/falmouth) or Hyannis for a week anchored around the beach. Private hibachi gives a group like that one shared night where everyone is at the same table, watching the same show, without anyone needing to cook or coordinate a restaurant reservation for a party of twenty.

Falmouth in particular has become a strong reunion market thanks to its large family-vacation rental inventory near Woods Hole and Falmouth Heights, which offers the kind of spacious properties that make hosting a big group dinner easy.

## Beach House Bachelorette and Group Weekends

Chatham's upscale Main Street and beach house rentals facing Nantucket Sound have made it one of the Cape's favorite bachelorette and group-weekend destinations. A private hibachi dinner the first night of the trip gives the group a shared, high-energy experience before the rest of the weekend's plans take over — no group reservation to coordinate, no waiting for a table at a restaurant that can't actually seat fourteen people together anyway.

## What to Expect from a Mobile Hibachi Event

**Fully self-contained setup:** your chef brings a propane teppan grill, so there's no need for gas hookups or special permissions from the rental property — this is the single biggest logistics question we get from vacation rental guests, and the answer is simple: we bring everything.

**All proteins cooked to order:** the standard menu plus premium upgrades — filet mignon, lobster tail, wagyu — available on request.

**The full show:** fire tricks, flying shrimp, and precision knife work, performed live for your group over 90–120 minutes.

**Complete cleanup:** your chef packs out everything when dinner is done. The rental is left exactly as it was.

**Group size:** one chef station comfortably serves up to 20–25 guests. For larger family reunions, we bring a second station.

## When to Book

Cape Cod's peak season is July and August — book 2–3 weeks ahead for those windows. Shoulder-season dates in June and September, while quieter, can often be booked with less advance notice. If your trip includes a specific date you can't move, reserving early is always the safer call, especially for larger group bookings.

**Pricing:** starting at $60 per adult, $30 per child (ages 4–12), $600 event minimum.
