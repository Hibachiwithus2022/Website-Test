The night before a North Shore wedding matters almost as much as the ceremony itself. Families are arriving from out of town, the wedding party is together for the first time all weekend, and the welcome dinner sets the tone for everything that follows. In [Marblehead](/locations/massachusetts/marblehead) and [Newburyport](/locations/massachusetts/newburyport), more of those welcome dinners are happening at a private waterfront property with a hibachi chef than at any restaurant in town.

## Why Waterfront Estates Are the Setting of Choice

Marblehead Harbor and the Merrimack River waterfront in Newburyport are lined with the kind of estate properties that make an outdoor wedding-weekend dinner feel effortless — a rear terrace overlooking the water, string lights already up for the weekend, and a setting no restaurant buyout can replicate. A private hibachi chef brings the full teppanyaki performance directly to that property: the grill, the proteins, the fire tricks, and a live show that becomes the story your wedding guests tell before the ceremony even starts.

Unlike a restaurant reservation, there's no capacity limit to negotiate and no risk of splitting your group across multiple tables. Everyone — the wedding party, the out-of-town family, the neighbors who've known the couple for twenty years — is at the same grill for the same two-hour show.

## Marblehead: Harborfront Rehearsal Dinners

Marblehead's sailing culture and Old Town waterfront estates have made it one of the North Shore's most requested wedding-weekend settings. A hibachi chef performing on a terrace overlooking Marblehead Harbor as the sun sets is, for a lot of families, the single most photographed moment of the entire weekend — more than the ceremony reception itself.

## Newburyport: Riverfront Welcome Dinners

Newburyport's compact, walkable downtown and Merrimack River waterfront properties draw wedding parties who want a rehearsal dinner within walking distance of Newburyport's boutique hotels and inns. With Plum Island and Crane Beach both minutes away, many wedding weekends here extend into a full beach day before or after the ceremony — and the hibachi dinner becomes the anchor event that brings the whole group together at one property.

## What to Expect from a Wedding Weekend Hibachi Event

**Premium proteins, standard for the occasion:** filet mignon, lobster tail, and wagyu upgrades are close to the default expectation for North Shore wedding-weekend dinners, not the exception.

**Fully self-contained setup:** your chef brings a propane teppan grill — no gas hookups required, whether you're hosting at a private home, a rental estate, or a wedding venue's outdoor space.

**The full show:** fire tricks, precision knife work, and every dish cooked to order, performed live for your group over 90–120 minutes.

**Complete cleanup:** your chef packs out everything when dinner is done, leaving the property spotless for the rest of the wedding weekend's events.

**Group size:** one chef station comfortably serves up to 20–25 guests. For larger wedding parties and family groups, we bring a second station.

## When to Book

North Shore wedding season (June through September) is the busiest booking window of the year for both towns — reserve 3–4 weeks ahead to secure a Friday or Saturday date. Off-season wedding weekends can often be booked with 2–3 weeks' notice.

**Pricing:** starting at $60 per adult, $30 per child (ages 4–12), $600 event minimum. Premium protein packages available at booking.
