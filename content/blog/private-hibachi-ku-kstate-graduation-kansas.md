# Private Hibachi for KU and K-State Graduation: Kansas City, Lawrence, and Manhattan

Commencement weekend at Kansas University (Lawrence) and Kansas State University (Manhattan) creates one of the most competitive restaurant reservation windows in the state. Families fly in from across the country, every private room at every restaurant books up months in advance, and the options for a group dinner that seats 15–25 people in a private setting are limited.

Private hibachi at your home or rental property solves this problem. A licensed teppanyaki chef comes to you — your Lawrence home near KU, your Manhattan house near K-State, or a Kansas City-area home for families hosting graduates from either campus — and performs the full hibachi dinner for your entire group. You're not competing for a reservation. The event is yours.

## The University Town Restaurant Problem

Lawrence and Manhattan are college towns. Their restaurant inventories are built around the student population and casual dining, not large-group private dining for commencement families. The few restaurants with private rooms — in both cities — fill their commencement-weekend slots in February and March.

This isn't a problem with booking early enough. It's a capacity problem. There simply aren't enough private dining options for the volume of commencement families that arrive in May.

Home hibachi bypasses the constraint entirely. You book a chef, not a restaurant table. You control the date, the menu, the guest list, and the timing.

## Lawrence: KU Graduation Season

Lawrence has 3,500–4,000 people earning degrees at KU each May. Many of those graduates have families traveling from out of state — Chicago, Denver, Atlanta, the coasts — who need a dinner option that can accommodate 12–25 people in a private setting on a weekend where every restaurant is at capacity.

Lawrence homes near the university — Rockledge, Sunset Hills, Clinton Parkway neighborhoods — typically have the yards and patios that work well for hibachi setups. Rental properties near campus that families book for the graduation weekend also work: the chef needs outdoor space and a power outlet, and most rental properties accommodate that.

**Best occasions in Lawrence:** KU commencement graduation parties, alumni reunion dinners, family milestone celebrations, end-of-season sports team dinners.

**Typical group sizes:** 14–26 guests.

**Booking note:** KU commencement is typically the second weekend of May. This is the highest-demand window — booking 6–8 weeks in advance for that weekend is strongly recommended.

**What Lawrence families say:** The most common feedback from KU graduation parties is that the home format let the family be fully present. No waiting, no noise from adjacent tables, no rush because the restaurant needs the table back. The performance gave everyone something to watch together, and the meal let them linger as long as they wanted.

## Manhattan: K-State Graduation Season

Manhattan has a similar dynamic with K-State commencement. The city is smaller than Lawrence, and its private dining inventory is even more limited. Families traveling from Wichita, Kansas City, Oklahoma, and out of state for K-State graduation find the same constraint: there's nowhere that can seat 20 people in a private room for a weekend dinner.

K-State's neighborhoods around Wildcat Creek, Scenic Drive, and the established west Manhattan residential areas have the homes and yards that accommodate hibachi setups well. The format also works for rental properties in the area.

Manhattan's college-town character means the graduation celebration culture is strong. Families host multiple-day gatherings, and the hibachi dinner often becomes the anchor event of the commencement weekend — the night before the ceremony or the celebration dinner after.

**Best occasions in Manhattan:** K-State commencement graduation parties, family reunion dinners, engineering and academic department team celebrations, alumni reunions.

**Typical group sizes:** 14–26 guests.

**Booking note:** K-State commencement is typically in mid-May. Book 6–8 weeks in advance for the best availability on that weekend.

**A note on timing:** Some Manhattan families host the hibachi dinner the evening before commencement — this avoids the post-ceremony rush and lets graduates and families enjoy the event without the day-of stress.

## Kansas City, Kansas: A Hub for Both University Markets

Kansas City, KS occupies a unique position in the Kansas university graduation market. It's within 45 minutes of Lawrence (KU) and about 90 minutes from Manhattan (K-State). Many families with graduates at either school have a home base in the KC metro — in KCK, in Johnson County, or across State Line Road in Missouri.

Hosting the graduation dinner in Kansas City rather than in the college town itself is a practical choice: more space, more familiar venue, easier parking, and better proximity for family members flying into KCI. For families with extended family coming from multiple directions, a KCK home in the Strawberry Hill, Argentine, or western Wyandotte County neighborhoods can be an ideal anchor point.

Beyond graduation season, Kansas City, KS generates hibachi demand from its strong blue-collar and professional communities. Corporate team events in KCK's logistics and transportation sector, neighborhood gatherings in its diverse residential communities, and family milestone celebrations are all well-established occasions.

**Best occasions in Kansas City, KS:** University graduation dinners for KU/K-State families, neighborhood block parties, corporate team events, milestone birthdays and anniversaries.

**Typical group sizes:** 16–30 guests.

**Service area:** The chef services all of Wyandotte County, Johnson County, and the greater KC metro area.

## What a University Graduation Hibachi Dinner Looks Like

**Before the chef arrives:** Set up tables and chairs in your outdoor space. Rectangular folding tables in a U-shape or two parallel rows work best. Plan for the grill station to anchor one end. Have drinks set up for when guests arrive.

**When the chef arrives (30 min before the performance):** The chef unpacks the portable grill station, verifies the protein orders for each guest, and arranges the cooking area. You don't need to do anything — the chef manages their own setup.

**The performance (60–90 min):** The full teppanyaki show: knife tricks, fire, the onion volcano, egg tricks, followed by cooking and serving everyone's individual orders. Guests sit together and watch, then eat together when food is served.

**After dinner:** The chef packs up all equipment and leaves. Your graduation celebration continues at its own pace — no restaurant asking for the table back.

## Booking for KU and K-State Graduation Season

Commencement dates vary by year, but both KU and K-State typically hold ceremonies in early-to-mid May. The calendar around those dates is the highest-demand window of the year for Kansas.

For commencement weekend bookings in Lawrence, Manhattan, or the KC metro:
- **Ideal booking window:** 6–8 weeks before the event
- **Minimum recommended:** 3 weeks before for the best selection of available dates

For all other Kansas events, 2–3 weeks advance notice is typically sufficient.
