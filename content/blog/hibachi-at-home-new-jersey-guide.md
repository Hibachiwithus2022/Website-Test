New Jersey is one of the most concentrated private event markets in the country. Dense suburbs, high household incomes, a multigenerational family culture with strong graduation and celebration traditions, and a Jersey Shore vacation rental scene that runs millions of visitors through beach houses every summer — it all adds up to a state where private hibachi has an enormous natural fit.

The challenge in New Jersey is logistics: it's a small, dense state with significant geographic variation between North Jersey's suburban commuter towns, the shore communities, and South Jersey's quieter residential markets. Here's how private hibachi works across the state.

## How Private Hibachi Works

A certified teppanyaki chef arrives at your outdoor space — your backyard, patio, or vacation rental — 30–45 minutes before your event. They bring everything: a propane teppan grill, all fresh proteins and ingredients, plates, sauces, utensils. They set up independently, run a 90–120 minute live cooking performance, and clean up completely at the end.

Pricing in New Jersey is **$60 per adult** and **$30 per child** (12 & under), with a **$600 event minimum**.

## North Jersey: Suburban Backyard Culture

The commuter towns of Morris, Essex, Union, and Bergen counties represent some of the best private hibachi territory in the Northeast. Towns like [Morristown](/locations/new-jersey/morristown), [Summit](/locations/new-jersey/summit), [Westfield](/locations/new-jersey/westfield), [Ridgewood](/locations/new-jersey/ridgewood), [Montclair](/locations/new-jersey/montclair), [Short Hills](/locations/new-jersey/short-hills), [Livingston](/locations/new-jersey/livingston), and [Chatham](/locations/new-jersey/chatham) all have large older homes with established backyards — mature trees, patio surfaces, the kind of outdoor spaces that make private events genuinely comfortable.

This is graduation country. New Jersey has one of the most intense graduation party cultures on the East Coast. May and June Saturdays in Morris and Essex counties are some of the heaviest-demand dates in our entire Northeast service area. If your graduation party is in one of these towns, book 6–8 weeks in advance for a June Saturday. Seriously.

Beyond graduation, North Jersey's wealthy suburban demographic produces consistent birthday, anniversary, and corporate entertaining demand. Households that commute to Manhattan use private hibachi as an easy way to do a genuinely impressive home entertaining event without the logistics of going into the city.

**Setup considerations in North Jersey:** Older colonial and Victorian homes in Summit, Westfield, and Ridgewood often have more compact rear yards than you'd expect from the front. Confirm your outdoor square footage — 10×10 feet minimum — when booking.

### Bergen County: Fort Lee, Hackensack, Paramus

[Fort Lee](/locations/new-jersey/fort-lee), [Hackensack](/locations/new-jersey/hackensack), and [Paramus](/locations/new-jersey/paramus) run a denser, more urban setup than Morris County. Townhouses and smaller single-family homes predominate. Hibachi works here when there's a usable patio or yard — confirm space carefully before booking.

## Central Jersey: Bridgewater, Marlboro, Holmdel

[Bridgewater](/locations/new-jersey/bridgewater), [Marlboro](/locations/new-jersey/marlboro), and [Holmdel](/locations/new-jersey/holmdel) sit in a sweet spot: newer construction than North Jersey with larger lot sizes, still accessible to the NYC metro, and an Indian-American and Korean-American community that embraces large family celebrations. Graduation season here is as intense as Morris County — plan equally early.

[Princeton](/locations/new-jersey/princeton) draws from the university calendar. Graduation weekends in May and large faculty/professional household entertaining events run year-round.

## The Jersey Shore: Vacation Rental Season

New Jersey's shore communities are a different beast entirely. [Asbury Park](/locations/new-jersey/asbury-park), [Point Pleasant](/locations/new-jersey/point-pleasant), [Belmar](/locations/new-jersey/belmar), [Long Branch](/locations/new-jersey/long-branch), [Ocean City](/locations/new-jersey/ocean-city), [Wildwood](/locations/new-jersey/wildwood), [Stone Harbor](/locations/new-jersey/stone-harbor), [Avalon](/locations/new-jersey/avalon), [Sea Isle City](/locations/new-jersey/sea-isle-city), [Cape May](/locations/new-jersey/cape-may), and [Spring Lake](/locations/new-jersey/spring-lake) all have strong weekly rental markets where groups want one special dinner event.

The shore format is straightforward: a rented beach house, 10–20 people, an outdoor deck or patio, one chef. The group stays at the house, the chef handles the dinner, and everyone gets an evening that doesn't require navigating the shore's packed restaurant scene at peak summer.

**Book shore dates as early as possible.** July and early August shore weeks book months in advance for rental properties. Lock in your hibachi date as soon as the rental is confirmed — 5–8 weeks out minimum for peak summer.

**Deck and patio setups:** Shore houses typically have good outdoor space. Front porches, rear decks, and backyard patios all work. Confirm the surface is flat and non-carpeted.

## South Jersey: Cherry Hill, Moorestown, Haddonfield

[Cherry Hill](/locations/new-jersey/cherry-hill), [Moorestown](/locations/new-jersey/moorestown), [Haddonfield](/locations/new-jersey/haddonfield), and [Mount Laurel](/locations/new-jersey/mount-laurel) round out New Jersey's residential market to the south. The event culture here is similar to North Jersey — graduation-heavy in June, birthday and anniversary parties year-round — with somewhat more relaxed booking windows than the intensity of Morris County June Saturdays.

## What's Included

- Certified teppanyaki chef with propane teppan grill
- Two proteins per person (chicken, steak, shrimp, scallops, salmon, tofu)
- Salad, fried rice, grilled vegetables, and house-made sauces
- All plates, utensils, and equipment
- Full setup and complete cleanup

**Upgrades:** Filet mignon (+$10/person), Lobster tail (+$15/person), Extra protein ($15/order), Yakisoba noodles ($5/order), Gyoza ($10).
