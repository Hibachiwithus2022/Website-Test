Colorado has five private hibachi markets that work differently from each other — and knowing which one you're in determines how far ahead you need to book, what occasions make the most sense, and what setup logistics look like at your property.

This is the complete guide to all five.

---

## Market 1: The South Denver Estate Corridor

Cherry Hills Village, Greenwood Village, and Lone Tree form Colorado's most concentrated estate private dining market. These are the communities built around the assumption that entertaining happens at home, at scale, with a level of quality that matches the property.

**[Cherry Hills Village](/locations/colorado/cherry-hills-village)** is the center of this market — Country Club Road, Cherryridge, and the Quincy corridor. Estate anniversaries, milestone birthdays, small holiday dinner parties, and corporate executive gatherings are the primary occasions. Premium proteins across the whole table (filet mignon, Chilean sea bass, lobster tail) are the standard, not the upgrade.

**[Greenwood Village](/locations/colorado/greenwood-village)** adds the DTC corporate dimension. Greenwood Village estate events run parallel to Denver Tech Center client appreciation dinners and team events — the same chef, the same self-contained setup, the same performance quality at a venue that's five minutes from the Interlocken corridor.

**[Lone Tree](/locations/colorado/lone-tree)** rounds out the south Denver estate market. RidgeGate estate dinners, Park Meadows-area celebrations, and Lone Tree milestone events are the primary formats.

**What to know about south Denver estate events:**
- Events book 3–5 weeks ahead in summer and holiday season
- Premium proteins (filet mignon, lobster tail, wagyu) are the most common upgrade in this market
- Groups of 8–20 are the most common size for estate dinners; corporate events often run 20–40

## Market 2: Denver Metro Corporate and Home Events

**[Denver](/locations/colorado/denver)** is the largest and most diverse Colorado market — Cherry Creek home anniversary dinners, Washington Park backyard graduation parties, LoDo rooftop corporate team events, Highlands neighborhood celebrations. A certified teppanyaki chef who travels to any Denver neighborhood with a fully self-contained setup.

The Metro market also includes:
- **[Broomfield](/locations/colorado/broomfield)** — Interlocken Business Park corporate events, DISH and Oracle campus team dinners, home celebrations across the tech corridor
- **[Arvada](/locations/colorado/arvada)** — Olde Town celebrations, Ralston Creek neighborhood backyard parties, northwest Denver graduation events
- **[Lakewood](/locations/colorado/lakewood)** — Green Mountain neighborhood parties, Bear Creek graduation celebrations, Belmar-adjacent backyard milestones

**The Denver metro booking pattern:**
- Same-day quotes are the standard for most metro events
- Holiday corporate party season (November–December) books 4–6 weeks ahead for all metro neighborhoods
- Summer graduation Saturdays (May–June) book 4–6 weeks ahead for suburban areas; Denver proper is slightly more available

## Market 3: Denver Suburban Graduation Season

The suburban Denver graduation market is the most volume-intensive in Colorado — and the most time-sensitive to book.

**[Highlands Ranch](/locations/colorado/highlands-ranch)** is the dominant graduation party market. Thunderridge, Rock Canyon, and Mountain Vista families generate consistent demand in May and June for Douglas County backyard graduation events. Large lots, outdoor-friendly communities, and the cultural expectation of a home celebration make Highlands Ranch one of the better-suited markets in the state for private hibachi.

**[Centennial](/locations/colorado/centennial)** runs a parallel graduation market for Cherry Creek School District families — Cherry Creek High School, Smoky Hill, Grandview, and Eaglecrest graduation parties on Arapahoe County residential lots.

**[Parker](/locations/colorado/parker)** adds the southeast Douglas County dimension — Chaparral, Legend, and Ponderosa High School graduation parties on the large residential lots Parker was built for.

**[Castle Rock](/locations/colorado/castle-rock)** has grown into Colorado's fastest-growing graduation party market — Terrain, Castle Oaks, and Castle Pines-adjacent celebration events.

**What makes the suburban graduation market different:**
- May and June Saturday evenings book 4–6 weeks ahead across all suburban markets
- Dual chef stations are strongly recommended for groups over 30 (common in graduation party format)
- The chef handles the evening so parents and grandparents can be present at the celebration instead of managing the kitchen

## Market 4: Boulder and the Northern Front Range

The Boulder–Fort Collins corridor runs on an academic calendar, and private hibachi booking windows follow it.

**[Boulder](/locations/colorado/boulder)** has CU Boulder commencement as its peak booking event — late May graduation weekend generates concentrated demand across South Boulder Road estates, Chautauqua-area properties, and vacation rental properties in the Flagstaff and Sunshine Canyon corridors. Outside graduation season, Boulder is a consistent tech exec home dinner and bachelorette rental market.

**[Fort Collins](/locations/colorado/fort-collins)** mirrors Boulder on the CSU calendar — late May CSU commencement is the peak demand window, followed by Old Town vacation rental bachelorette weekends and Horsetooth Reservoir-adjacent home celebrations throughout summer.

**[Louisville](/locations/colorado/louisville)** offers Boulder County access with consistently better availability — Old Town Louisville home celebrations and Coal Creek corridor estate dinners book 2–3 weeks ahead even in peak season.

**The Front Range booking pattern:**
- CU and CSU graduation weekends (typically third or fourth Saturday of May): book 5–7 weeks ahead
- Summer vacation rental bachelorette events: book 2–4 weeks ahead
- Off-season: typically confirm 1–2 weeks out

## Market 5: Colorado Springs and the South Front Range

**[Colorado Springs](/locations/colorado/colorado-springs)** runs on a different calendar than the Denver metro — and the occasions are distinct. Military homecoming celebrations at Fort Carson, Peterson Space Force Base, and Schriever Space Force Base. USAFA graduation week family dinners. Broadmoor neighborhood estate events. Garden of the Gods-adjacent corporate retreats.

The military homecoming market is the most unique private hibachi occasion in Colorado. The format — the chef comes to your home, performs for 20–40 family members, everyone eats at the same time — works particularly well for homecoming celebrations where the focus should be on the returning service member, not on restaurant logistics.

**The USAFA graduation dimension:**

USAFA graduation week is the most concentrated booking window in the Colorado Springs market. Families fly in from across the country — multiple events happen within the same long weekend. If your USAFA graduation dinner falls on commencement weekend, book 6–8 weeks ahead.

## Market 6: Colorado Mountain Destinations

The mountain resort market is where the private hibachi logistics question matters most: **Can a chef set up at a ski rental?**

Yes. The chef arrives with a fully self-contained propane teppan grill — no gas hookup, no outdoor kitchen, no permanent infrastructure required. Any flat deck, patio, or outdoor surface at any Colorado mountain property works.

**[Aspen](/locations/colorado/aspen):** Peak season (June–September, December–March) books 6–10 weeks ahead. Snowmass Village chalet events, Red Mountain estate dinners, and Aspen Mountain slopeside rentals.

**[Vail](/locations/colorado/vail):** Peak ski season and summer festival dates book 4–8 weeks ahead. Vail Village chalet dinners, Lionshead ski-in rentals, and Beaver Creek estate celebrations.

**[Breckenridge](/locations/colorado/breckenridge):** Ski weekends and summer dates book 5–8 weeks ahead. Summit County's most visited ski town, with consistent demand for bachelorette weekends, family ski reunions, and birthday group rentals.

**[Steamboat Springs](/locations/colorado/steamboat-springs):** Ski season and summer cabin weekends book 4–6 weeks ahead. Yampa Valley ranch rentals and Fish Creek-adjacent vacation homes.

**[Telluride](/locations/colorado/telluride):** Festival weekends and peak ski season book as early as possible — Telluride is a high-demand destination where dates move fast.

**The mountain logistics checklist:**
- Tell your chef about access at booking: steep driveways, gated community entry, unpaved roads, weight-limited bridges
- Propane is self-contained — no hookup needed at any Colorado mountain rental
- Setup takes 20 minutes on any flat outdoor surface
- Peak season mountain dates should be reserved the same week you confirm the rental

## Standard Pricing Across All Colorado Markets

- **$60 per adult** · **$30 per child** (ages 4–12) · children 3 and under eat free
- **$600 event minimum** (most residential events meet this easily)
- **Premium upgrades:** filet mignon, lobster tail, Chilean sea bass, king shrimp, wagyu beef — per-guest, selected at booking
- **Mountain travel:** factored into quotes for remote mountain properties — always disclosed at booking
