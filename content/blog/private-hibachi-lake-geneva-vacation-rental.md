Lake Geneva vacation rentals are ideal for private hibachi for three specific reasons:

**Self-contained propane setup.** Our teppan grill runs on its own propane system. There's no gas hookup needed — no outdoor gas line, no grill hookup at the property, no special equipment required. Shore Path waterfront decks, Grand Geneva-adjacent vacation homes, Williams Bay lakefront properties, and Fontana-on-Geneva-Lake cottages are all equally suitable. If your rental has a flat outdoor area of 10×10 feet, we can set up.

**The group is already together.** The best thing about a vacation rental dinner is that nobody has to drive anywhere. Everyone is at the property. The chef arrives, sets up, and the evening starts — no parking, no wait for a table, no half the group arriving late. For a bachelorette weekend of twelve, a family reunion of thirty, or a corporate retreat of twenty, the shared table experience is exactly what the occasion needs.

Your vacation rental host's requirements for property condition at checkout are the same whether we were there or not — we pack out completely, leaving no trace. The only things we ask you to provide are seating and dinnerware for your group.

## How to Book a Lake Geneva Vacation Rental Event

**Step 1: Confirm your rental.** Once your Lake Geneva property is booked and your weekend dates are confirmed, reach out immediately. Summer Saturday dates fill our calendar 8–10 weeks ahead — the longer you wait after your rental is confirmed, the higher the risk your chef date is unavailable.

**Step 2: Share your details.** Guest count, property address, preferred dinner time, and any dietary requirements. We confirm availability within hours.

**Step 3: Lock in your date.** A deposit confirms your booking and holds your date. No changes are possible once the property's other guests have made plans around the dinner — book early.

**Step 4: Confirm your menu.** 48–72 hours before the event, finalize your protein choices. Standard menu includes chicken, steak, shrimp, and salmon. Premium upgrades — filet mignon, lobster tail, wagyu beef, king shrimp — are available at a per-guest rate and are common at Lake Geneva events.

**Step 5: The chef arrives.** 30–45 minutes before your requested start time, the chef arrives to set up. The full teppanyaki performance runs 90–120 minutes from first flame to final plate. When dinner is done, we break down and pack out completely.

## What to Tell Your Vacation Rental Host

Most Lake Geneva vacation rental hosts have no restrictions on propane grills — our setup is functionally identical to a portable propane grill, which most properties allow. If your property has a "no open flames" policy or a strict propane restriction, contact us before booking and we'll advise on whether the setup works at your specific property.

We recommend letting your host know in advance that a private chef service will be on-property — not because it's required, but because it removes any ambiguity. We're a licensed, insured, professional service, and most hosts are happy to accommodate once they understand what we do.
