North Carolina's graduation season is one of the Southeast's most concentrated private hibachi booking windows. The state has four major research universities — UNC, Duke, NC State, and Wake Forest — plus a large network of community colleges and professional schools, all in close proximity in the Research Triangle and Piedmont Triad. Families hosting graduation dinners for 20 to 50 guests at Triangle-area homes represent a consistent, high-volume annual market.

## Triangle Universities

**University of North Carolina – Chapel Hill** commencement runs in mid-May. The surrounding communities — [Chapel Hill](/locations/north-carolina/chapel-hill), Carrboro, Pittsboro, and southern Durham County — have residential areas with suburban lot profiles. The neighborhoods north and east of UNC's campus, particularly in [Cary](/locations/north-carolina/cary) and [Apex](/locations/north-carolina/apex), have newer construction with excellent outdoor setups for graduation event hosting.

**Duke University** commencement in Durham in mid-May draws families from across the country. Durham's residential areas (Hope Valley, Forest Hills, Trinity Park) have varied lot sizes. The western Durham suburbs and Chapel Hill corridor have more consistent suburban profiles.

**NC State University** commencement in [Raleigh](/locations/north-carolina/raleigh) runs in mid-May. The North Raleigh suburbs (North Hills, Wakefield, Leesville Road corridor) and the Cary/Apex/Holly Springs corridor have excellent residential properties with large lots. This is the highest-volume graduation event corridor in the Triangle.

**Wake Forest University** in Winston-Salem has its commencement in mid-May. The [Winston-Salem](/locations/north-carolina/winston-salem) residential communities (Ardmore, Buena Vista, Sherwood Forest) have varied lot sizes; the suburban areas in Kernersville and High Point have more consistent setups.

## Charlotte Metro Graduation Season

The Charlotte metro has a large network of universities and colleges — UNC Charlotte, Davidson College, Johnson C. Smith University, Queens University — that generate graduation demand throughout Mecklenburg and surrounding counties.

**Davidson College** in Davidson, north of Charlotte, has an early May commencement that draws families throughout the Lake Norman corridor.

**UNC Charlotte** commencement in mid-May draws families throughout the Charlotte metro, with the North Charlotte, Concord, and Huntersville corridors being the strongest areas.

## The Triangle Family Profile

The Triangle's tech and research economy (Research Triangle Park, pharma, biotech, state government) has drawn a large South Asian and East Asian professional community. This population drives some of the highest graduation event volumes in the Southeast — families hosting 30 to 60 guests for a graduation dinner are common in [Cary](/locations/north-carolina/cary), [Apex](/locations/north-carolina/apex), and [Holly Springs](/locations/north-carolina/holly-springs).

**Dual-station setup:** For graduation parties of 35 or more guests, a dual-station setup (two chefs) dramatically improves the experience. Book dual-station if your guest count is projected above 35.

## Booking Windows

- **4–5 weeks out:** Charlotte metro and Triad (Winston-Salem) during May
- **3–4 weeks out:** Outside peak May window
