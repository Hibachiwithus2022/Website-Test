New Jersey graduation season is one of the most concentrated private hibachi booking windows in the Northeast. The state has an unusually high density of top-performing public high schools, major research universities, and medical and law schools — and the families here, particularly in the affluent North and Central Jersey suburbs, treat graduation celebrations as significant occasions. Hosting 25 to 60 guests for a backyard graduation dinner with a private hibachi chef is a standard format in Livingston, Short Hills, Westfield, Cherry Hill, and the Edison-to-Princeton pharma corridor.

## University Graduation Season

**Rutgers University** — New Brunswick commencement runs in mid-May, with individual school ceremonies spread over multiple days. Families hosting post-ceremony dinners on the same weekend should book 5 to 6 weeks out.

**Princeton University** — Princeton commencement is in early June. The surrounding residential communities (Plainsboro, West Windsor, Lawrenceville) host consistent graduation event demand that weekend.

**[Montclair State University](/locations/new-jersey/montclair)**, **Seton Hall University** (South Orange), and **NJIT** (Newark) have May graduation ceremonies that drive demand throughout Essex, Union, and Morris counties.

**New Jersey medical schools** (Rutgers New Jersey Medical School, Rutgers Robert Wood Johnson Medical School, Hackensack Meridian School of Medicine) have May graduation ceremonies that bring families from across the state for celebration dinners.

## High School Graduation Season

New Jersey's top-ranked public high schools — many of which rank nationally — graduate in late May and early June. These schools' communities have among the highest per-household incomes in the country and strong graduation party cultures:

**North/Central Jersey:** Westfield High School, Summit High School, Millburn High School (Short Hills), Livingston High School, Ridgewood High School, Bergen County Academies, Montgomery High School (Princeton area), South Brunswick High School (Edison corridor).

**Graduation dinner format:** A typical NJ graduation dinner runs 25 to 50 guests, often combining immediate family with extended family and close family friends. Backyard properties in Westfield, Summit, Short Hills, Livingston, and Ridgewood almost universally have adequate outdoor space. Edison and Piscataway have more varied lot sizes — confirm dimensions.

**South Jersey:** Cherry Hill East and West High Schools, Moorestown High School, Haddonfield Memorial High School, Marlboro High School, Holmdel High School.

## Dual-Station Setup for Large Groups

For graduation parties of 35 or more guests, a dual-station setup (two chefs, two grills running simultaneously) dramatically improves the experience — it cuts the cooking time in half and maintains the interactive performance format for everyone. Book dual-station if your guest count is projected above 35.

## Planning Timeline

- **5–6 weeks out:** North/Central Jersey (Livingston, Short Hills, Westfield, Summit, Edison) during peak May–June season
- **4–5 weeks out:** South Jersey (Cherry Hill, Moorestown, Holmdel) during May–June
- **3–4 weeks out:** Outside peak May–June season
