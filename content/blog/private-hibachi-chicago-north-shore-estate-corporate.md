## Chicago Corporate: River North, Fulton Market, West Loop, and Beyond

Chicago's corporate private hibachi market is among the most active in the Midwest — a city-wide professional ecosystem that spans financial services, technology, healthcare, real estate, and every industry in between.

**Why private hibachi outperforms corporate restaurant dining.** A River North private dining room seats your group at individual tables for a plated experience that is professionally executed and completely passive. Private hibachi at your event venue or team member's property — any flat outdoor area of 10×10 feet, no gas hookup required — gives your group a shared, interactive, live-fire experience that creates genuine engagement across the full group for 90–120 minutes. Corporate teams that have done both consistently describe the hibachi format as the first team dinner that felt like it actually belonged to the team rather than to the restaurant.

**What Chicago corporate events typically look like:**
- Groups of 20–60 guests; dual stations for groups above 25
- Venue flexibility: outdoor loft terraces, office courtyards, hotel rooftop spaces, and private home properties all work
- Premium proteins available for client entertainment tiers — filet mignon, lobster tail, Chilean sea bass, wagyu
- Setup in 20–30 minutes; performance runs 90–120 minutes; full cleanup when dinner ends

**The Chicago corporate calendar:** Q4 holiday team events (November–December) fill 4–6 weeks ahead. Q3 appreciation events (August–October) book 3–4 weeks ahead. Q1 and Q2 are typically more available. Same-day quotes always available for any date.
