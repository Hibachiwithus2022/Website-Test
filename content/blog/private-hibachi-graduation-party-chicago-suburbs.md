If you're planning a graduation party in the Chicago suburbs, you've discovered something: the weekend of your student's commencement is the same weekend as every other graduation in your school district, and every restaurant within fifteen minutes of the school has been booked by other families since March.

The solution most Chicago-area families have landed on is the same one: a private hibachi chef at your home. The chef arrives at your property with a fully self-contained propane teppan grill, all proteins, and everything needed for a 90–120 minute live performance for 25–60 guests. You don't manage any logistics during the party. You attend it.

Here's how it works across the six Chicago suburbs with the most active graduation markets.

## Naperville: The DuPage County Graduation Capital

Naperville North and Naperville Central are two of Illinois's largest high schools — and they graduate within days of each other in late May. When both schools hold commencement in the same week, the demand for private graduation dinner formats in Naperville concentrates on exactly four or five Saturdays in late May and early June.

The [Naperville](/locations/illinois/naperville) graduation market runs on some of the state's most beautiful residential properties: White Eagle, Ashwood Park, Cress Creek, Hobson West, and the neighborhoods along the Naperville Riverwalk corridor whose backyards can accommodate the forty-family-member gatherings that Naperville graduation parties typically require.

**What a Naperville graduation party looks like in practice:** Your chef arrives 20–30 minutes before start time with the propane teppan grill, all proteins, fried rice, vegetables, miso soup, salad, house-made sauces, and all plates and utensils. For groups of 30–50, dual stations run simultaneously so everyone eats at the same time and watches the same performance. Fire tricks, the onion volcano, flying shrimp — the live show keeps every age group at the table for the full duration. Filet mignon or lobster tail upgrade for your graduate is the most common add-on we run at Naperville events.

**Booking window:** Naperville graduation Saturdays fill 4–6 weeks ahead. Book in early April for any late May date. Same-day quotes are always available — but availability on peak Saturdays requires advance commitment.

## Wheaton: College Town and High School Graduation Market

[Wheaton](/locations/illinois/wheaton) is one of the few Illinois communities with both a significant college graduation market (Wheaton College) and a dual high school graduation market (Wheaton Warrenville South, Wheaton North). All three hold commencement in the same three-week window.

The Wheaton graduation party market skews slightly larger in group size than Naperville's — Wheaton College graduation weekends draw family members from across the country, and the backyard gatherings in Briarcliffe, Silver Lake, and the Cantigny-corridor neighborhoods tend toward 35–55 guests.

Cantigny Park-adjacent properties in Wheaton are some of the finest outdoor entertaining spaces in DuPage County. If your backyard has access to a large flat area — any standard suburban lot works, the chef needs 10×10 feet of clearance — you have everything needed for a full dual-station graduation party.

**What's different about Wheaton College graduation:** Family members often travel from significant distances, which makes Friday-evening events nearly as active as Saturday. If your student's commencement is Saturday, a Friday dinner for the arriving family is a common format — and availability on the Friday before graduation weekend is typically better than the Saturday.

**Booking window:** DuPage graduation Saturdays — 4–6 weeks ahead. Wheaton College commencement weekend — 5–7 weeks ahead for peak Saturday bookings.

## Downers Grove: Two High Schools, One Graduation Window

Downers Grove North and Downers Grove South graduate within days of each other — which means the [Downers Grove](/locations/illinois/downers-grove) graduation party market has the same concentrating effect as Naperville's, compressed into an even smaller geographic footprint.

The Belmont Road and Fairview Avenue residential corridors, the downtown Downers Grove neighborhoods, and the Lyman Woods-adjacent properties are all standard settings for Downers Grove graduation parties. The typical event is 30–50 guests with dual stations and the filet mignon upgrade for the graduate.

One thing that distinguishes Downers Grove from Naperville: the downtown walkability creates a temptation to make a restaurant reservation and then realize, two weeks before graduation Saturday, that the reservation is for eight people and you have forty relatives. Most Downers Grove families who book private hibachi describe discovering the format after exactly this experience.

**Booking window:** DG North and South graduation Saturdays — 4–6 weeks ahead. Book in late March for any May date.

## Highland Park: Ravinia Season and North Shore Graduation

[Highland Park](/locations/illinois/highland-park) has a graduation market and an entirely separate summer entertaining market — and they overlap on the same calendar.

Highland Park High School graduation falls in late May or early June, which is also when Ravinia season opens. The same backyard on the same May Saturday might be a graduation party or a Ravinia-adjacent garden dinner depending on whose home it is. The point is that May and June backyard availability in Highland Park is more limited than any other month in the calendar.

The Highland Park graduation party market is distinctive in two ways: first, the proximity to Lake Michigan means that lakefront properties and Ravinia-corridor estates are the common setting, giving these events a backdrop that DuPage County graduation parties don't have. Second, Highland Park High School graduation tends toward larger guest lists — 40–55 family members is common — which makes the dual-station format effectively standard.

**North Shore graduation booking note:** The entire North Shore — Highland Park, Lake Forest, Winnetka, Evanston, Northbrook — has graduation concentrated in the same late-May/early-June window. All these communities compete for the same chef availability. Book early.

**Booking window:** Highland Park graduation Saturdays — 4–6 weeks ahead. Ravinia season events — 3–5 weeks ahead. Lakefront properties in peak summer — 3–4 weeks ahead.

## Northbrook: Glenbrook North and the North Shore Family Market

Glenbrook North graduation in [Northbrook](/locations/illinois/northbrook) is one of the North Shore's most established graduation traditions — and the Northbrook backyard graduation party market reflects the community's size and wealth.

Techny Prairie-area neighborhood properties, the Northbrook Court-adjacent residential communities, and the Chicago Botanic Garden corridor homes in Northbrook and nearby Glencoe host graduation parties for 35–50 family members every late May and early June. The typical Northbrook graduation party format is identical to the rest of the North Shore: dual stations, standard proteins plus a premium upgrade for the graduate, full performance, full cleanup.

What distinguishes Northbrook is its position: the community sits between the North Shore estate corridor (Lake Forest, Winnetka, Evanston) and the Cook County family suburbs (Schaumburg, Barrington). The graduation party culture has absorbed characteristics of both — the North Shore expectation for quality and the family suburb expectation for scale.

**Booking window:** Glenbrook North graduation Saturdays — 4–6 weeks ahead. Standard Northbrook events — 2–3 weeks out.

## Orland Park: Southwest Suburbs Graduation Market

[Orland Park](/locations/illinois/orland-park) is the Southwest Suburbs' largest graduation market — Carl Sandburg High School is one of the biggest high schools in suburban Cook County, and the residential lot sizes in Orland Park make it one of the most naturally suited communities in the Chicago metro for private backyard events.

The Lake Sedgewick-area neighborhoods, the Centennial Park-adjacent residential communities, and the Orland Park Crossing corridor properties are common graduation party settings. Groups of 35–50 are the norm. Dual stations are the standard setup.

What makes Orland Park distinctive from the North Shore and DuPage markets: the Southwest Suburbs have fewer private chef alternatives per capita than either corridor, which means that the graduation weekend restaurant reservation problem is more acute here. Families who discover the private hibachi format in Orland Park almost universally describe it as the most obvious solution to a problem they'd been managing badly for years.

**Booking window:** Carl Sandburg and Orland Park graduation Saturdays — 4–6 weeks ahead. Book in early April for any May date.

## Logistics: What Every Chicago Suburbs Graduation Party Needs

**Space:** 10×10 feet of flat outdoor clearance per station. Standard suburban backyards, patio decks, and driveways all work. For dual stations, plan for two 10×10 areas adjacent to each other, or one larger cleared area.

**Seating:** You provide tables and chairs. The chef brings everything else.

**Group size:** Single station for 25 guests or fewer. Dual stations for 26–60 guests. Both stations run simultaneously — everyone eats at the same time.

**Proteins:** Standard menu includes chicken, steak, shrimp, and salmon. Premium upgrades — filet mignon, lobster tail, Chilean sea bass — available per guest. Most Chicago-area graduation families add filet mignon for the graduate and a few key family members; the option is per-person, not all-or-nothing.

**Children:** Ages 4–12 are $30 per child. Ages 3 and under eat free. The fire tricks and flying shrimp are the highlight of the evening for younger guests.

**Timing:** Chef arrives 20–30 minutes before start. Performance runs 90–120 minutes. Full cleanup when dinner ends.
