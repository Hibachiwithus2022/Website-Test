Washington state has two distinct private hibachi markets that share the same outdoor culture but little else. The Puget Sound side — [Seattle](/locations/washington/seattle), [Bellevue](/locations/washington/bellevue), [Tacoma](/locations/washington/tacoma) — is a dense, tech-heavy, affluent metro where backyard entertaining runs April through October in a compressed season that people take seriously. Eastern Washington — [Spokane](/locations/washington/spokane) — is a quieter mid-sized city with a strong university and family culture and an outdoor season that runs hot and dry through summer.

Both markets support private hibachi well. Here's how it works across the state.

## What Private Hibachi at Home Means

Hibachi Connect sends a certified teppanyaki chef to your outdoor space — your backyard, patio, or private venue. They arrive 30–45 minutes before the event with a propane teppan grill, all fresh proteins, vegetables, sauces, plates, and utensils. They set up independently, run a 90–120 minute live cooking performance for your guests, and clean up completely at the end. You need a flat outdoor surface of at least 10×10 feet.

Pricing is **$60 per adult** and **$30 per child** (12 & under), with a **$600 event minimum**.

## The Seattle Metro and the Eastside

[Seattle](/locations/washington/seattle) and the surrounding Eastside — [Bellevue](/locations/washington/bellevue), Kirkland, Redmond, Mercer Island, Issaquah — represent one of the most concentrated pools of private event demand in the Pacific Northwest. The tech industry (Amazon, Microsoft, Tableau, Expedia) has brought enormous wealth to the region along with a transplant population from California, New York, and Asia that expects quality and is accustomed to private chef experiences.

**Who books on the Eastside:** Tech industry households entertaining clients or teams. Indian-American and Chinese-American communities in Bellevue and Redmond with strong graduation and family celebration culture. Affluent households in Medina, Clyde Hill, and Mercer Island using private hibachi for intimate dinner parties.

**Seattle proper setup notes:** Seattle's older neighborhoods (Capitol Hill, Fremont, Green Lake, Phinney Ridge) have smaller lots with less predictable outdoor space. Confirm square footage carefully for in-city bookings. The newer neighborhoods in North Seattle and the Eastside suburbs have more reliably generous outdoor setups.

## Tacoma and South Puget Sound

[Tacoma](/locations/washington/tacoma) has a different character from Seattle — more working-class roots, genuine neighborhoods, and a strong military presence from Joint Base Lewis-McChord. The private hibachi market here runs family celebrations, graduation dinners, and military homecoming events. Tacoma's residential areas have varied lot sizes; many South Tacoma, North End, and suburban Pierce County properties have good outdoor setups.

## Vancouver, Washington

[Vancouver](/locations/washington/vancouver) — the Washington city across the Columbia River from Portland, Oregon — benefits from both markets. It's essentially part of the Portland metro in terms of geography but serves a Washington audience that often prefers avoiding Oregon's income tax on earnings. Similar outdoor season and event culture as Portland.

## Spokane

[Spokane](/locations/washington/spokane) is Eastern Washington's largest city and home to Washington State University's medical school campus and Gonzaga University. Graduation season in May is consistent. Spokane's residential areas have more generous lot sizes than the Puget Sound side — standard suburban backyards with flat surfaces are common, and the valley areas east of the city have particularly good outdoor setups.

**Spokane's outdoor season:** Summers are hot and dry — ideal for outdoor events. Evenings cool quickly even in July and August. Plan events for 6:30–7:00 PM starts in peak summer. Spring (May–June) and fall (August–September) are prime.

**Upgrades:** Filet mignon (+$10/person), Lobster tail (+$15/person), Extra protein ($15/order), Yakisoba noodles ($5/order), Gyoza ($10).

## Washington Outdoor Season

The Puget Sound outdoor season runs May through September with July and August being the most reliably dry. Book 4–5 weeks out for summer Saturdays. Spokane and Eastern Washington has a drier, hotter summer and runs April through October.
