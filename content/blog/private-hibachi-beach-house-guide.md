A beach house weekend with a private hibachi chef is the format that ends every debate about where to eat dinner. No fighting for a reservation at a coastal restaurant in peak season. No driving back from dinner on a dark beach road. The chef comes to the property, sets up on the deck, and cooks for everyone in the same place where the weekend is already happening.

Here's how it works.

---

## The Setup: Deck vs. Backyard

**Deck:**
The most common beach house setup. The chef positions the propane teppan grill at one end of the deck; guests arrange seating around it. Works well for groups of 8–20.

For groups over 20 on a deck, note your count at booking — most beach house decks accommodate it, but the chef will plan service timing accordingly.

**Backyard / ground level:**
Better for larger groups (25–40+) who want more space. Ground-level setup on any flat outdoor surface — lawn, patio, paver area. The performance works exactly the same as a deck.

**Covered porch or lanai:**
Adds weather protection. The most comfortable option when temperatures or wind are a factor. Confirm ceiling height at booking — most covered porches handle cooking clearance fine; a particularly low roof overhang can restrict the full setup.

---

## Wind and Weather

Outdoor cooking at a beach house is exposed to coastal conditions in a way a suburban backyard isn't. Here's what to know.

**Wind:** A self-contained propane teppan grill manages wind better than an open grill because the flame is low and controlled. A covered porch or a deck with a windscreen resolves most concerns. If your property is directly oceanfront and exposed, note it at booking — the chef will factor it into setup placement.

**Rain:** Light rain on a covered porch or lanai is manageable. An uncovered outdoor setup in a significant rainstorm is not ideal. If your event date has a weather concern, flag it when you book — rescheduling options exist.

**Cold weather:** The chef brings the heat. Cold temperatures on a New England or Great Lakes coast aren't a barrier — the propane grill creates a localized heat zone, and the performance runs the same regardless of ambient temperature.

## Coastal Markets Where Beach House Hibachi Is Most Active

**Rhode Island South County:**
[Narragansett](/locations/rhode-island/narragansett) and [Westerly](/locations/rhode-island/westerly) have the highest beach house rental density in Rhode Island. Ocean House–area events, Narragansett Town Beach–adjacent rentals, and Misquamicut beach house weekends. Book 3–4 weeks ahead for July and August Saturdays.

**Newport, Rhode Island:**
[Newport](/locations/rhode-island/newport) vacation rentals and estate properties on Bellevue Avenue and Ocean Drive. Bachelorette beach house weekends are the most common Newport format — groups of 12–20, premium protein upgrades, Saturday dinner as the anchor event. Book 4–6 weeks ahead.

**South Shore and North Shore Massachusetts:**
[Hingham](/locations/massachusetts/hingham) and [Cohasset](/locations/massachusetts/cohasset) on the South Shore; [Newburyport](/locations/massachusetts/newburyport) and [Marblehead](/locations/massachusetts/marblehead) on the North Shore — harbor-adjacent home events and summer rental dinners.

**Cape Cod:**
[Hyannis](/locations/massachusetts/hyannis), [Chatham](/locations/massachusetts/chatham), and [Falmouth](/locations/massachusetts/falmouth) — Cape Cod vacation rental events. The summer beach house market here is consistent and concentrated; book 3–5 weeks ahead for July and August.

**South Carolina Coast:**
[Myrtle Beach](/locations/south-carolina/myrtle-beach) vacation rental events — the highest-volume beach rental market on the East Coast south of New Jersey. Large group formats are common. Also active: [Charleston](/locations/south-carolina/charleston), where the Folly Beach, Isle of Palms, and Sullivan's Island rental markets generate consistent private dinner events.

**Virginia Beach:**
[Virginia Beach](/locations/virginia/virginia-beach) oceanfront vacation home events and North End residential properties. The most active coastal hibachi market in Virginia.

**Lake Michigan Shore:**
[South Haven](/locations/michigan/south-haven) and [Saugatuck](/locations/michigan/saugatuck) — the West Michigan beach house rental corridor. Weekend cottage events, bachelorette lakeside dinners, and family reunion gatherings. Book 3–5 weeks ahead in summer.

**Maryland Shore:**
[Ocean City](/locations/maryland/ocean-city) — the most active vacation rental market on the Maryland coast. Ocean City bay-side rental houses and single-family homes north of downtown serve the format well; high-rise condo balconies generally don't have the 10×10 clearance needed.

## Group Sizes at a Beach House

| Group | Setup | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| 8–20 | One chef station | Standard beach house dinner; most decks accommodate this comfortably |
| 20–35 | One chef, adjusted timing | Chef plans service so everyone eats |
| 35+ | Two stations recommended | Dual setup means simultaneous service; note count at booking |

The most common beach house format is a bachelorette or family reunion weekend at 14–22 guests — one chef, one station, everyone seated around the grill.

## What the Chef Brings

- Propane teppan grill — fully self-contained, no gas hookup required at any beach house
- All proteins: chicken, steak, shrimp, salmon, vegetables, fried rice, lo mein, miso soup, salad
- House-made sauces: ginger, yum yum, teriyaki
- All plates, chopsticks, and utensils
- Full property cleanup — the chef leaves with everything

**Premium upgrades** (most popular at beach house events):
- Filet mignon
- Lobster tail
- Chilean sea bass
- King shrimp
- Wagyu beef

The most common upgrade pattern at beach house events: lobster tail and filet mignon for the celebration guest (bride-to-be, birthday person, milestone honoree), standard proteins for the rest of the group.
