Los Angeles is six distinct private hibachi markets stacked inside one metro area — and the occasion, the setting, and the expectation look entirely different in each one.

The [Beverly Hills](/locations/california/beverly-hills) estate anniversary dinner for sixteen guests on a north terrace in the Bel Air adjacencies is not the same event as the [Manhattan Beach](/locations/california/manhattan-beach) Strand graduation dinner for thirty family members two houses from the water. The [Malibu](/locations/california/malibu) Carbon Beach event where wagyu and lobster tail are the assumed default is not the same format as the [Pasadena](/locations/california/pasadena) Caltech corporate team event for twenty-eight employees. The [Calabasas](/locations/california/calabasas) Oaks estate milestone party operates on a different booking calendar than the [Santa Monica](/locations/california/santa-monica) vacation rental gathering near the pier.

This guide covers all six Los Angeles markets: what makes each distinct, what occasions drive private hibachi demand there, and when to book.

## Beverly Hills: Rodeo Drive Corridor, Bel Air Adjacencies, and North Beverly Hills

[Beverly Hills](/locations/california/beverly-hills) is the most active luxury estate private hibachi market in California. A community of Rodeo Drive residential corridor properties, Bel Air adjacency compounds on Benedict Canyon, North Beverly Hills estate homes above Sunset, and Beverly Hills flats properties where private dinners have been hosted at the highest level for generations.

A certified teppanyaki chef arrives at your Beverly Hills estate with a fully self-contained propane teppan grill, premium proteins as the baseline expectation, and a performance that transforms your outdoor terrace, pool deck, or courtyard into the finest private dining room in Los Angeles for the evening.

**The Beverly Hills estate event format:**
- Filet mignon, lobster tail, Chilean sea bass, and wagyu available per guest — these are not upgrade conversations; they are the expectation
- Dual chef stations for events of 20–40 guests; single station for intimate estate dinners of 10–18
- Complete self-contained setup in 20 minutes, live performance for 90–120 minutes, full cleanup when dinner ends — your estate is exactly as it was

**What Beverly Hills hosts book private hibachi for:**
Anniversary estate dinners on private terraces. Milestone birthday luxury celebrations. Executive client appreciation estate events for C-suite professionals who have attended private dinners at the highest level in cities across the country and find the live hibachi format genuinely different. Celebrity corridor landmark occasions. Holiday intimate estate dinner parties.

**When to book:** Beverly Hills estate dates fill 4–6 weeks ahead in peak season. Standard dates confirm 3–4 weeks out. Same-day quotes always available.

## Malibu: Carbon Beach, Point Dume, Malibu Colony, and Serra Retreat

[Malibu](/locations/california/malibu) is California's most iconic beach estate private event market — a PCH community of front-row ocean properties, Point Dume bluff homes, Malibu Colony residences, and Serra Retreat compounds where the properties are already the most extraordinary venues in Southern California before any chef sets foot on the deck.

A private hibachi chef arrives at your Malibu property with everything included — the self-contained propane teppan grill, filet mignon and lobster tail as standard upgrade options, fried rice, vegetables, house-made sauces, and complete cleanup — so the occasion happens exactly where it belongs rather than at a Malibu restaurant that every guest in the group already knows.

**What Malibu properties book private hibachi for:**
Carbon Beach front-row estate anniversary dinners as the sun drops into the Pacific. Point Dume bluff home graduation celebrations for family arriving from New York, Chicago, and London. Malibu Colony milestone birthdays where the chef sets up on the pool deck and the evening unfolds exactly as the setting always suggested it could. Serra Retreat-area compound events where premium proteins and an extraordinary outdoor setting are the two things any format needs to match simultaneously.

**When to book:** Malibu Carbon Beach and peak summer estate dates fill 4–6 weeks ahead. Standard Malibu dates confirm 3–4 weeks out.

## Calabasas and Hidden Hills: The Oaks, Calabasas Estates, and the Las Virgenes Corridor

[Calabasas](/locations/california/calabasas) is California's most concentrated gated community private hibachi market — a city of estate neighborhoods, gated enclaves, and a private entertaining culture that has been built around the expectation that the format matches the property rather than compromises with it.

The Oaks of Calabasas anniversary dinner for eighteen guests behind a gated entry. Calabasas Estates graduation party for forty-two family members — the chef sets up on the pool deck, dual stations for the full group, filet mignon and lobster tail upgrades across the table, fire tricks that had even the skeptical grandparents completely transfixed. Hidden Hills-adjacent luxury estate events where the community's standard for private dining means premium proteins and a performance that keeps every guest at the table from start to finish.

**What Calabasas and Hidden Hills hosts book private hibachi for:**
Gated community anniversary estate dinners. Calabasas High graduation parties for large multigenerational family groups. Milestone birthday celebrations at Hidden Hills-adjacent properties. Executive home events in the Las Virgenes Road corridor where the chef arrives at the gate and the occasion belongs entirely to the property.

**When to book:** Calabasas gated community estate dates fill 4–6 weeks ahead in peak season. Graduation Saturdays in May and June book 4–6 weeks out.

## Santa Monica: Ocean Park, the Palisades, and Vacation Rentals Near the Pier

[Santa Monica](/locations/california/santa-monica) is the LA beach community market that bridges the estate world and the vacation rental world — Ocean Park bungalow backyard graduation parties, Palisades-adjacent anniversary estate dinners for sixteen guests on a private terrace, vacation rental gatherings near the pier for out-of-town family groups who need the evening to stay at the property rather than scatter across the Third Street Promenade restaurant corridor.

A certified teppanyaki chef travels to your Santa Monica property or vacation rental with a fully self-contained propane teppan grill — no gas hookup, no outdoor kitchen required. For groups of 25–50, dual stations available. Complete setup, full performance, full cleanup.

**When Santa Monica fills up:** Graduation Saturdays in May and June for Santa Monica College and high school commencements. Summer vacation rental events. Standard Santa Monica dates book 3–5 weeks ahead; graduation season — 4–6 weeks out.

## Manhattan Beach: The Strand, Tree Section, and South Bay Graduation Season

[Manhattan Beach](/locations/california/manhattan-beach) is South Bay's most active graduation party market — a Strand estate community, Tree Section neighborhood backyard culture, and Sand Section family milestone calendar that collectively produce more May and June private hibachi bookings than any other South Bay community.

The Strand estate graduation dinner for thirty family members on a deck two houses from the water. Tree Section anniversary dinner for eighteen guests in a backyard where the Pacific is four blocks away. Sand Section milestone birthday party for thirty-six guests where the chef sets up dual stations and every generation — from 8-year-old nieces to 72-year-old grandparents — is completely captivated simultaneously.

Manhattan Beach graduation Saturdays in May and June book fast — often 4–6 weeks ahead by mid-March for the most popular Saturdays. If you're planning a Mira Costa or Manhattan Beach High graduation event, submit your date as soon as the commencement calendar is set.

Rose Bowl corridor estate anniversary dinners for twenty guests on west-facing terraces with the Arroyo Seco as backdrop. San Rafael Hills graduation backyard parties for forty-five family members on June Saturdays. Caltech and JPL corporate team events at Pasadena-area properties where a live hibachi chef produces something that no Old Town Pasadena restaurant private dining room can replicate.

**When Pasadena fills up:** Graduation Saturdays in May and June (Pasadena City College, Caltech commencement, local high school commencement Saturdays) fill 4–6 weeks ahead. Corporate events run year-round and typically confirm 2–3 weeks out.
