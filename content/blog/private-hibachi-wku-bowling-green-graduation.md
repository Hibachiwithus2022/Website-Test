# Private Hibachi for WKU Graduation in Bowling Green, Kentucky

Western Kentucky University commencement is one of the most concentrated restaurant-booking events in south Kentucky. Bowling Green is a mid-size university town with WKU drawing students from across Tennessee, Indiana, Kentucky, and the broader Mid-South — and its restaurant capacity fills months in advance for May graduation weekend. Private hibachi catering at your Bowling Green home solves the problem before the scramble ever starts.

## The WKU Graduation Restaurant Problem

Here's what most WKU graduation families face:

**The capacity problem.** Bowling Green has excellent local restaurants — Mariah's, Rick's White Light Diner, Spencer's Coffee — but none of them have private rooms that hold 20 family members flying in from Nashville, Indianapolis, and across Tennessee. The ones that come close fill up fast.

**The driving problem.** The closest metro with a full private group dining market is Nashville, 65 miles south on I-65. That's a 90-minute round trip on a graduation weekend when the whole family is already in Bowling Green.

**The occasion problem.** A graduation that took four years, family members from four states, and a milestone that deserves its own event — shouldn't end at a crowded restaurant. Private hibachi at your home creates a shared experience that no restaurant can match for a group your size.

## WKU Graduation Booking Guide

### Spring Commencement (May)

WKU's spring commencement typically falls in early to mid-May. This is the highest-demand graduation weekend in Bowling Green by a significant margin.

**Key booking facts:**
- Bowling Green restaurants for groups of 15+ are booked by February or March for May commencement
- Private hibachi dates for WKU weekend fill several weeks ahead — book by late February for May events
- Most WKU graduation bookings are for families staying in Bowling Green for the full weekend

### Fall Commencement (December)

WKU also holds a December commencement. Demand is significantly lower than May, but Bowling Green's already limited restaurant capacity still fills up. Book 3–4 weeks ahead for December events.

## Planning Your Bowling Green Event

### Guest count and timing
- Standard events: 10–28 guests
- Chef arrives 20–30 minutes before event start
- Event duration: 90–120 minutes
- Cleanup: 15–20 minutes after dinner

### What you need to provide
- Flat outdoor surface at least 10×10 feet
- 15 feet of overhead clearance
- Access to a water source for cleanup
- Guests seated within 6–8 feet of the grill

Most Bowling Green suburban properties — covered patios, pool decks, backyard lawn setups — meet all four requirements without modification.

### Menu for WKU graduation parties
For a WKU graduation party of 18–22 guests, a popular format is:
- **Graduate's choice:** One premium protein upgrade (filet mignon or lobster tail)
- **Family standard:** Steak and shrimp combination
- **Kids at the table:** Chicken

The chef takes individual orders at the grill — no prix-fixe required.
