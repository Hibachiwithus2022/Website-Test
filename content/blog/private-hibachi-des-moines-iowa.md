[Des Moines](/locations/iowa/des-moines) has emerged as one of the Midwest's more dynamic mid-sized cities — a financial services capital (Principal Financial, Nationwide Insurance) with a growing tech presence and a backyard entertaining culture that takes summer seriously. Private hibachi works well here: the event format translates directly to the large suburban backyards that characterize the metro's residential development.

## Des Moines Neighborhoods

**West Des Moines and Waukee:** The western suburbs are the highest-income corridor in the metro. Waukee is one of Iowa's fastest-growing communities — newer construction, generous lot sizes, covered patio setups standard in higher-end developments.

**Ankeny:** North of Des Moines along I-35, Ankeny has strong family event culture. Graduation parties and milestone birthday celebrations run consistently through spring and summer.

**Johnston and Urbandale:** Upper-middle-income suburbs with established residential profiles. Good outdoor setups throughout.

**Des Moines proper:** Older urban neighborhoods with smaller lots. Confirm outdoor square footage carefully for bookings in the city core.

## What Works Well in Des Moines

Graduation parties for Drake University, Grandview University, and Des Moines Area Community College families (May and June). Corporate team events from insurance and financial services firms. Summer birthday milestone parties through the outdoor season.
