## How It Works in Ottawa

A certified teppanyaki chef arrives at your outdoor space — backyard, patio, venue, or rental property — 30–45 minutes before your event start. They bring a propane teppan grill, all fresh ingredients, plates, sauces, utensils, and everything else needed to run a complete live hibachi dinner. You need one thing: a flat outdoor surface with at least 10×10 feet of usable space.

The chef sets up independently, runs a 90–120 minute performance while cooking and serving your guests in stations, and handles full cleanup when dinner is finished. You don't need a kitchen, prep space, or catering equipment. The setup works in Ottawa backyards from late May through early October.

## Ottawa Neighbourhoods and Setup

Ottawa's residential variety — from the dense older neighbourhoods of Centretown and Glebe to the wide suburban lots of Barrhaven, Kanata, and Orleans — means the setup experience varies significantly.

**Westboro and Glebe:** Older, narrower lots. Most homes have workable outdoor spaces but may need some arrangement planning. Semi-detached homes with shared fences can still work if the chef's station fits the yard's layout. Always confirm square footage if you're in a denser neighbourhood.

**Kanata and Barrie (south and west Ottawa):** Newer suburban subdivisions with larger, more open backyards. These are some of the easiest setups in Ottawa — rectangular yards, level surfaces, and open sightlines for guests. Dual-station events of 30+ guests tend to run smoothly in these areas.

**Orleans:** Similar to Kanata in lot size and format. Orleans has a strong francophone community and runs a similar graduation and family celebration calendar. May and June are the busiest booking windows here.

**Barrhaven:** Larger newer homes with generous outdoor space. Barrhaven backyards frequently have enough room for dual-station setups and easy parking access for the chef's vehicle.

## Outdoor Season in Ottawa

Ottawa's outdoor entertaining season runs roughly May through September, with June and July being the peak months. October can work on warmer evenings with the right space — confirm weather expectations when booking fall dates.

For June graduation party bookings and Canada Day weekend events, book 5–6 weeks in advance. Summer Saturdays in Ottawa fill earlier than most people expect. Off-peak windows (May, August weekdays, September) can typically be arranged with 2–3 weeks notice.
