## Why Graduation Parties Drive Oklahoma's Private Hibachi Calendar

Oklahoma graduation events share a consistent structure: the guest list is 18–28 people, the family has come from two or three different states, and the dinner needs to feel like it matches the occasion. A restaurant reservation that splits the group across two tables, requires a prix-fixe menu, or puts grandparents from Wichita next to a stranger's bachelorette party does not match the occasion.

Private hibachi at your home:
- Keeps every guest at the same table for the full 90-minute dinner
- Allows individual protein orders — no prix-fixe, no sharing conflict
- Delivers a live performance that makes dinner an event in itself
- Eliminates parking, wait times, and corkage fees
- Scales from 12 to 40 guests at the same address

The chef arrives with the full self-contained setup, performs, and handles cleanup. Your graduation guest list — however large, however geographically scattered — is seated together for the best dinner of graduation weekend.

## University of Oklahoma: Norman Commencement

**When it happens:** OU commencement typically falls in May, with multiple ceremonies across undergraduate, graduate, and professional programs. Exact dates vary year to year — check the OU Academic Calendar for the current year's schedule.

**Why it's challenging:** Norman graduation weekend is the single most concentrated restaurant demand event in the south OKC corridor. Norman restaurants — along with every OKC option within 30 minutes — fill by February for any date within a week of commencement. Groups of 20 or more have essentially no restaurant options.

**Private hibachi for OU graduation:** Private hibachi works perfectly in Norman. Campus-area homes, south Norman suburban properties, and OKC-area homes all work for the format. A family that wants every relative at the same table for their OU graduate's dinner should book 5–7 weeks before commencement weekend — earlier if they have a specific Saturday night.

**Typical OU graduation event:** 20–26 guests, mix of parents, siblings, grandparents, and the graduate's closest friends. Two or three proteins (chicken, steak, shrimp are common; filet mignon is a popular upgrade for this milestone). Duration: approximately 100 minutes.

## Oklahoma State University: Stillwater Commencement

**When it happens:** OSU holds commencement in May and December. The May ceremony draws the larger crowd; the December ceremony is often overlooked but creates the same restaurant-booking problem in a compressed winter window.

**Why it's challenging:** Stillwater is a smaller city than Norman, which means the restaurant capacity per graduate is even lower. Every downtown Stillwater restaurant is fully booked within days of OSU releasing the commencement calendar.

**Private hibachi for OSU graduation:** Stillwater rental homes, campus-area properties, and longer-drive family homes in the OKC corridor all work. Family groups traveling in for OSU commencement from Texas, Kansas, Colorado, or Missouri should book 5–7 weeks before the May ceremony and 3–4 weeks before December.

**Typical OSU graduation event:** 18–24 guests, similar structure to OU events. The December OSU commencement often works with a covered patio or garage setup — Oklahoma's November and December weather can be variable, and the self-contained propane setup doesn't require outdoor conditions.

## Cameron University: Lawton Commencement

Cameron University commencement in Lawton creates a private dining event for Fort Sill-area families and Southwest Oklahoma communities. Lawton's restaurant options are limited relative to the graduation demand, and the military community here has a distinct preference for private, controlled events. Private hibachi at your Lawton home is a strong fit. Book 3–4 weeks ahead.

## OKC Metro High School Graduation

Oklahoma City metro high school graduation runs from late April through late May. Demand concentrates heavily in the first two weekends of May.

**Edmond:** Heritage Hall and Casady graduate some of Oklahoma's highest-achieving students, and the graduation parties that follow are large and formally organized. Edmond executive households regularly host 24–30 family members for graduation weekend. Heritage Hall graduation parties should be booked 5–7 weeks ahead.

**Yukon and Moore:** Yukon High School and the Moore/Westmoore district graduate a large combined student body in the first two weeks of May. OKC west and south metro graduation parties should be booked 4–5 weeks ahead.

**Midwest City:** Midwest City High School graduation aligns with the broader OKC metro peak. Tinker AFB families and east metro households should book 4–5 weeks ahead.

**Norman high schools:** Norman North and Norman High graduation falls in the same window as OU commencement — which means the restaurant scramble in Norman is even more acute in May. Private hibachi at a Norman home is the practical solution for both university and high school graduation parties in the same week.

## Tulsa Metro High School Graduation

Tulsa metro graduation runs from late April through mid-May, with Broken Arrow High School, Owasso High School, Jenks High School, and Bixby High School all anchoring large graduation party circuits in the South and North Tulsa growth corridor.

**Broken Arrow:** BA High School is one of the largest high schools in Oklahoma. Graduation parties here regularly bring 20–25 family members together, with relatives coming in from Texas and Kansas. The Rose District area and BA's newer neighborhoods have strong outdoor entertaining infrastructure. Book 5–6 weeks ahead.

**Owasso and Jenks:** Both Owasso High and Jenks High School are in rapidly growing communities with excellent outdoor space. Book 4–5 weeks ahead for graduation season.

**Bixby:** Bixby High School graduation falls in the same Tulsa metro window. Bixby's properties — particularly the newer pool deck communities — are well-suited for private hibachi graduation dinners. Book 4–5 weeks ahead.

## What to Include in Your Oklahoma Graduation Event

**Proteins:** For a graduation party with a mixed guest list (grandparents, siblings, parents, grad's friends), chicken and steak are safe anchor proteins. Shrimp is popular. Filet mignon or lobster tail upgrades are worth it for this milestone — expect roughly $10–15 per guest for a full filet upgrade.

**Guest count:** Tell us the count when you book. For Oklahoma metro events, 20–28 guests at a single-chef setup is the norm. Groups above 30 typically benefit from a two-chef setup.

**Outdoor vs. covered:** Oklahoma May weather is variable. A covered patio, pergola, or garage setup works for rain contingency. If you have outdoor space that might be weather-exposed, have a covered backup plan.

**Surprise elements:** Several Oklahoma graduation hosts book without telling the graduate about the chef until arrival. The setup takes 20–30 minutes — have your graduate occupied elsewhere or plan the reveal as part of the event.
