Central New Jersey runs along the Route 1 and I-287 corridors — a pharmaceutical and tech industry belt that stretches from Princeton north through Edison, Bridgewater, and Morristown. The private hibachi market here is defined by two overlapping audiences: the pharma and biotech industry that employs a large South Asian professional community, and the established commuter suburbs with high incomes and strong outdoor entertaining cultures.

Hibachi Connect serves [Princeton](/locations/new-jersey/princeton), [Edison](/locations/new-jersey/edison), [Morristown](/locations/new-jersey/morristown), [Bridgewater](/locations/new-jersey/bridgewater), [Chatham](/locations/new-jersey/chatham), [New Brunswick](/locations/new-jersey/new-brunswick), and [Middletown](/locations/new-jersey/middletown).

## Princeton

[Princeton](/locations/new-jersey/princeton) has two distinct markets. Princeton University graduation in June draws families hosting post-commencement dinners for 20 to 40 guests — a consistent annual window. The town's residential neighborhoods have large lots and significant wealth from the university, finance, and tech communities.

The area surrounding Princeton — Plainsboro, West Windsor, East Windsor — has a large Indian-American population from the pharma and tech corridors. High school and college graduation events here are among the highest-volume in central New Jersey.

## Edison and the Route 1 Corridor

[Edison](/locations/new-jersey/edison) has one of the largest Indian-American communities in the United States — a fact that translates directly into private hibachi demand. Graduation parties (high school, medical school, engineering), family milestone celebrations, and community events for 30 to 60 guests are standard formats.

The residential neighborhoods throughout Edison and adjacent Woodbridge have suburban lot profiles with generally good outdoor setups. Confirmation of outdoor square footage is recommended for older housing stock in densely developed sections.

## Morristown and the Morris County Corridor

[Morristown](/locations/new-jersey/morristown) is Morris County's historic center — an affluent suburb with significant pharmaceutical company headquarters (Pfizer, Allergan, Cognizant) and a strong executive residential community. The neighborhoods surrounding downtown Morristown have large properties with well-designed outdoor spaces.

[Bridgewater](/locations/new-jersey/bridgewater) is a corporate hub (SANOFI, Synchronoss, Verizon) with suburban residential development that extends through Somerset County. Good outdoor setups throughout.

[Chatham](/locations/new-jersey/chatham) is one of Morris County's most prestigious residential addresses — a wealthy commuter suburb with large lots, strong outdoor entertaining culture, and event expectations that match.

## Middletown and Monmouth County

[Middletown](/locations/new-jersey/middletown) is Monmouth County's largest township — a large suburban community with varied residential profiles ranging from waterfront properties to standard suburban lots. Graduation and family event demand is consistent.
