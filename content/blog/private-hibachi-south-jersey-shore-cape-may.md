South Jersey has two distinct markets. The inland suburbs — [Cherry Hill](/locations/new-jersey/cherry-hill), [Moorestown](/locations/new-jersey/moorestown), [Mount Laurel](/locations/new-jersey/mount-laurel), [Haddonfield](/locations/new-jersey/haddonfield) — are established Philadelphia-area commuter communities with strong family event cultures. The Shore — [Asbury Park](/locations/new-jersey/asbury-park), [Ocean City](/locations/new-jersey/ocean-city), [Stone Harbor](/locations/new-jersey/stone-harbor), [Cape May](/locations/new-jersey/cape-may), [Wildwood](/locations/new-jersey/wildwood), [Sea Isle City](/locations/new-jersey/sea-isle-city), [Avalon](/locations/new-jersey/avalon), [Spring Lake](/locations/new-jersey/spring-lake) — is a classic East Coast beach rental market drawing Philadelphia, South Jersey, and broader Mid-Atlantic vacation groups.

## Cherry Hill and the Philadelphia Suburbs

[Cherry Hill](/locations/new-jersey/cherry-hill) is South Jersey's most commercially active municipality — a residential suburb directly across the Delaware River from Philadelphia with a large Jewish community and strong family event culture. Bar and bat mitzvahs, graduation dinners, and milestone birthday parties for 20 to 50 guests are consistent formats.

**[Moorestown](/locations/new-jersey/moorestown)** and **[Haddonfield](/locations/new-jersey/haddonfield)** are two of South Jersey's most historic and affluent residential communities — mature tree-lined neighborhoods with large lots and properties built for entertaining. These are ideal private hibachi setups.

**[Mount Laurel](/locations/new-jersey/mount-laurel)** is a large planned community with consistent suburban lot profiles and strong family event demand. Good outdoor setups throughout.

**[Marlboro](/locations/new-jersey/marlboro) and [Holmdel](/locations/new-jersey/holmdel)** in Monmouth County have large residential lots and strong South Asian community graduation event demand — Holmdel in particular has a substantial Indian-American population with consistent high school graduation party volume.

## The Jersey Shore

The Jersey Shore is one of the East Coast's most active beach rental markets. Groups from Philadelphia, South Jersey, and the broader Mid-Atlantic rent houses sleeping 10 to 20 people for a week at a time, and private hibachi gives them one exceptional dinner night.

**[Asbury Park](/locations/new-jersey/asbury-park)** has undergone significant revitalization — the vacation rental stock here now draws a younger, more food-focused crowd from Brooklyn and Philadelphia. Birthday celebration weekends and friend group trips are the core market.

**[Spring Lake](/locations/new-jersey/spring-lake)** is the upscale alternative to Asbury Park — a historic Victorian shore town with large properties and an older affluent demographic. Private dinner parties at Spring Lake rentals tend toward the more formal end.

**[Long Branch](/locations/new-jersey/long-branch), [Belmar](/locations/new-jersey/belmar), [Point Pleasant](/locations/new-jersey/point-pleasant)** are family-focused Shore communities with active rental markets. Multigenerational family weeks with 15 to 25 guests are common.

**The Cape May and Stone Harbor corridor** — [Cape May](/locations/new-jersey/cape-may), [Stone Harbor](/locations/new-jersey/stone-harbor), [Avalon](/locations/new-jersey/avalon), [Sea Isle City](/locations/new-jersey/sea-isle-city) — is the premium end of the Jersey Shore rental market. Properties here are larger, the demographic is more affluent (many Philadelphia Main Line families), and private hibachi works well for the dinner-party format that this market gravitates toward.

**Shore setup note:** Ocean breezes can be significant — confirm wind protection for the outdoor cooking area. Upper decks exposed to the ocean wind need a natural or built windbreak.
