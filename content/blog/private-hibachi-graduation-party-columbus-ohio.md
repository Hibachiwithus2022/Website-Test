## Why Private Hibachi Outperforms Every Columbus Restaurant Option

The Columbus-area graduation party creates a specific logistical problem that restaurants aren't built to solve:

**The group is too large for most private dining rooms.** A typical Columbus graduation party runs 25–60 guests. That's above the threshold for most restaurant private dining rooms, and the ones that can accommodate it require a prix fixe menu, a minimum spend, and a room that feels like a banquet hall rather than a celebration.

**The family wants flexibility.** Guests arrive in waves. Grandma is vegetarian. Cousins are gluten-free. The graduate wants Wagyu. Restaurant menus don't accommodate this without becoming a logistical headache.

**The timing has to be perfect.** Graduation ceremonies end at different times. Pre-parties run long. Restaurant reservations don't flex.

A private hibachi chef at home solves all three. The chef arrives at your home, sets up in your backyard, and starts when your family is ready. The menu is customized to every guest. The group size scales with your headcount. And when it's over, the chef packs out completely — no venue coordinator to negotiate with, no minimum spend overage.

[Dublin](/locations/ohio/dublin) graduation season is one of our busiest booking windows of the year — and Coffman, Jerome, and Dublin Scioto ceremony weekends fill our calendar fast.

The Dublin graduation party typically follows a specific format: ceremony Saturday morning, quick family photos at Muirfield, and then a backyard party at home that starts mid-afternoon and runs into the evening. A private hibachi chef for the dinner portion gives parents something to anchor the celebration around — not just another backyard cookout, but an experience the graduate's 35–45 guests will talk about.

Bridge Park-area condo owners and Historic Dublin homeowners with more limited outdoor space: we've done it all. A flat 10×10 area on a patio or driveway is all we need to set up the full grill.

**Dublin graduation booking window:** Reserve 4–6 weeks ahead of your ceremony date. May graduation Saturdays in Dublin fill by early April in most years.

## Planning Your Columbus-Area Graduation Party

### Step 1: Lock Your Date Before You Plan Anything Else

This is the single most important piece of advice for Columbus graduation season. Once you know your graduate's ceremony date, reserve the hibachi chef before you do anything else — before you order the cake, before you send invitations, before you finalize the guest list.

May graduation Saturdays in Dublin, New Albany, Powell, Upper Arlington, Beachwood, and Hudson compete for the same pool of chefs. The families who book in March get their first choice. The families who call in late April often find their preferred Saturday already filled.

### Step 2: Know Your Guest Count (and Round Up)

For pricing and logistics, give us your best headcount estimate — and round up by 10–15% for guests who confirm late. It's much easier to accommodate a few extra guests than to turn people away.

For groups over 25: we bring two chefs and two grill stations. Both run simultaneously, so the whole group eats at the same time rather than in shifts. Mention your anticipated count when you inquire.

For groups over 50: we can deploy three chefs. Mention this at booking and we'll build a logistics plan.

### Step 3: Build the Menu Around Your Graduate

The menu is fully customizable. Standard proteins — chicken, shrimp, salmon, and steak — are included at the base price. Premium upgrades include:

- **Wagyu beef** — the most popular upgrade for Columbus luxury suburb graduation parties
- **Filet mignon** — a cleaner cut for guests who prefer it
- **Lobster tail** — popular for milestone graduation dinners
- **Sea scallops and Chilean sea bass** — requested frequently in Beachwood and New Albany

Dietary accommodations (vegetarian, gluten-free, shellfish-free) are handled individually at no extra charge. Just list your needs at booking.

### Step 4: Prep Your Outdoor Space

A flat 10×10 area is the minimum for a single chef station. You don't need to move furniture, rent equipment, or prepare anything — the chef arrives with everything including the grill, all food, all utensils, and cleanup supplies.

For parties over 25 with two chef stations, confirm you have approximately 10×20 feet of outdoor clearance. Deck, patio, driveway, or grass all work equally well.
