## Why Private Hibachi Works for Madison Graduation Parties

**The restaurant reservation problem is solved.** Every Madison restaurant worth booking for a graduation dinner is fully reserved 6–8 weeks before Commencement. The families who wait until April to plan a May Saturday dinner are calling restaurants that stopped taking reservations in March. Private hibachi has no reservation problem — your event is at your property, and the chef comes to you.

**Every guest eats together.** Restaurant graduation dinners inevitably involve split tables — the graduate's immediate family at one table, the visiting relatives at another, the graduate's friends at a third. Private hibachi gathers everyone around one teppan grill for one shared meal and one shared performance. For a group of twenty-five to forty-five family members who've traveled from across the country for this weekend, the shared table is the point.

**The setting is already meaningful.** The home where the graduate grew up. The Nakoma backyard where they played. The Shorewood Hills patio where the family has gathered for every milestone. Private hibachi honors that setting — the chef arrives at your property and brings the restaurant-quality experience to where the evening should happen.

**The performance is unforgettable.** Precision knife work, flame tricks, flying shrimp, live teppanyaki cooking from first flame to final plate. For graduation parties that span multiple generations — grandparents who've never seen teppanyaki, graduate's friends who came from their college apartment, younger siblings who've been looking forward to the fire tricks for months — private hibachi is the format that works for every age simultaneously.

## Planning Your UW-Madison Graduation Dinner

### The Right Timing

UW-Madison Commencement typically falls on the second weekend of May. The Saturday before and the Saturday of Commencement are our two most-booked dates in all of Dane County. We recommend:

- **Booking 6–8 weeks ahead** for any UW-Madison graduation Saturday
- **Booking 4–6 weeks ahead** for surrounding graduation-adjacent weekends (Mother's Day weekend, Memorial Day weekend)
- **Contacting us in early April** if your date is any May Saturday in the Madison area

The families who book in March get their preferred date and preferred dinner time. The families who call in May are often looking at Friday evenings or Sunday afternoons — not because we don't want to serve them, but because the Saturday calendar fills completely.

### Group Size and Setup

Most UW-Madison graduation parties run 20–45 guests. Here's what works at each size range:

**Under 25 guests:** Single chef station. The performance is intimate — everyone can see the chef from their seat, and the cooking time keeps pace with conversation. 10×10 feet of flat outdoor space (patio, deck, backyard lawn, driveway) is all that's needed.

**25–40 guests:** Dual chef stations recommended. Two chefs, two teppan grills, everyone eating at the same time. Note your headcount at booking — dual-station events are staffed differently and require a larger outdoor footprint (approximately 10×20 feet of flat space).

**40+ guests:** Contact us directly to discuss. We staff large events with a team and set up a configuration that fits your property.

### The Menu

Standard menu includes chicken, steak, shrimp, salmon, fried rice, noodles, vegetables, miso soup, garden salad, and house-made sauces. Premium upgrades are available per guest:
- **Filet mignon** — the most common upgrade for graduation events
- **Lobster tail** — paired with filet at milestone dinners
- **Wagyu beef** — for events where premium is the standard
- **King shrimp** — popular upgrade for seafood-forward guests

Dietary accommodations — gluten-free, vegetarian, allergen-specific — are handled at the ingredient level. Specify at booking.

### Setting Up at Your Madison Property

Our setup needs: a flat outdoor area of 10×10 feet (single station) or 10×20 feet (dual station), outdoor access for the chef to bring equipment from the street or driveway, and a power outlet within 30 feet if your event starts after sunset (for lighting — not for cooking, which is propane). 

Common settings that work well: 
- Nakoma and Shorewood Hills backyard patios
- Isthmus-adjacent home outdoor areas
- Maple Bluff estate properties
- Waunakee, Middleton, and Verona suburban backyard patios
- Sun Prairie and Fitchburg residential properties
