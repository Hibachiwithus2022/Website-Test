Park City runs on a different calendar than the rest of Utah. Ski season peaks in January, when the Sundance Film Festival fills every restaurant reservation in town for two weeks straight. Summer brings a second wave of mountain-biking trips, corporate retreats, and destination weekends. Year-round, the guests staying in [Park City](/locations/utah/park-city)'s ski estates, Deer Valley chalets, and Old Town rentals expect the same standard at dinner that they came here to find on the mountain.

A private hibachi chef solves the one problem every Park City host runs into: there's no restaurant in town that can seat your whole group on short notice during peak season, and even when there is, it's not the same as having the entire trip's best night happen at your own property.

## Why Sundance Season Books Out Every Restaurant in Town

Sundance Film Festival week is the single most competitive dining window in Utah. Every restaurant on Main Street is booked solid weeks in advance — often with buyout events for studios and sponsors — and last-minute reservations for a group of ten or more are close to impossible.

Private hibachi sidesteps the problem entirely. Your chef arrives at your ski property with a fully self-contained setup — teppan grill, propane, all ingredients — and performs for your group on your own deck, in your great room, or on a covered patio. No reservation, no waiting for a table, no competing with festival crowds. Just your group, the chef, and a two-hour show built around the film-festival energy of the week.

**Book 4–6 weeks ahead for Sundance week** — this is the one date on the Park City calendar where waiting until the last minute simply won't work.

## Bachelor and Bachelorette Weekends in Old Town

Old Town's historic Main Street rentals are a favorite for bachelor and bachelorette weekends — walkable to the bars and restaurants, but private enough for the group to have a night that's just theirs. A private hibachi dinner the first night of the trip has become one of the most requested formats for exactly this reason: it gives the group a shared, high-energy experience before the rest of the weekend's plans take over, and it means nobody has to coordinate a group reservation at a restaurant that can't actually seat fourteen people together anyway.

Fire tricks, flying shrimp, and a chef who plays to the room — it's built for a group that wants the night to be about them, not about finding a table.

## Corporate Retreats Near Canyons Village

Corporate teams booking offsite retreats near Canyons Village increasingly skip the restaurant buyout entirely. A private hibachi dinner at the retreat property gives the whole team the same table, the same chef, and the same shared moment — which does more for a team than a formal dinner ever does. It also solves the logistics problem of moving a group of twenty or thirty people to and from a restaurant in the evening after a full day of meetings or activities.

## Ski Estate and Deer Valley Chalet Dinners

For hosts entertaining at a ski-in/ski-out estate near Deer Valley or a chalet along the Jordanelle Reservoir corridor, private hibachi is simply the highest standard of in-home dining available in the market. Premium protein upgrades — filet mignon, lobster tail, wagyu — are close to the default expectation here rather than the exception, and our chefs are equipped for the cold-weather logistics that come with a mountain property: covered decks, heated patios, and setups that work regardless of what the forecast says.

## What to Expect When You Book

**Your chef brings:** a self-contained propane teppan grill (no gas hookups required — critical for ski properties and rental condos), all proteins cooked to order, premium upgrades available on request, fried rice, vegetables, house-made sauces, and full serving setup.

**What you provide:** a flat outdoor or covered area of at least 10×10 feet — a deck, patio, or great room with adequate ventilation — and seating for your group.

**The format:** setup takes about 20 minutes, the performance runs 90–120 minutes from first flame to final plate, and your chef handles complete cleanup before leaving. For groups over 25, we bring a second chef station.

**Pricing:** starting at $60 per adult, $30 per child, $600 event minimum. Premium protein packages available at booking.
