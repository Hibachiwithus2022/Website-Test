[Spokane](/locations/washington/spokane) and [Tacoma](/locations/washington/tacoma) are Washington's second cities — different in character from Seattle and from each other, but both strong markets for private backyard hibachi events. Spokane is Eastern Washington's university and regional hub city with a dry, hot summer and generous residential lots. Tacoma is South Puget Sound's working-class heart with strong military ties and a family event culture built on significant occasions.

## Spokane: Eastern Washington's Summer Market

[Spokane](/locations/washington/spokane) has a character that Western Washington residents sometimes miss: it's a genuine mid-sized American city with affordable housing, big residential lots, and a backyard culture that takes summer seriously. When summer arrives in Spokane — hot, dry, and sunny — the outdoor entertaining season is on.

**The Spokane audience:** Gonzaga University and Washington State University's medical campus generate graduation event demand through May and June. Spokane's South Hill and Indian Trail neighborhoods have large newer homes with excellent outdoor setups. The Valley — Spokane Valley, Cheney Road corridor — runs consistent family event demand through the summer.

**What makes Spokane work for private hibachi:**

*The outdoor season runs hot and dry.* Unlike Western Washington, Spokane summers are reliably sunny with low humidity. July and August evenings are warm through 9–10 PM. This is genuine backyard party weather.

*Lot sizes are generous.* Spokane's residential areas have more room than Seattle proper. Standard suburban backyards in the South Hill, Indian Trail, and Valley neighborhoods meet the minimum requirements easily.

*University graduation culture.* Gonzaga's graduation in May draws large family celebrations. WSU's medical and law programs generate consistent graduate-level celebrations through spring.

**Spokane setup notes:** The South Hill's older neighborhoods (above 29th Avenue) have varied terrain — confirm backyard flatness. The South Hill's newer development areas, along with the Valley and North Spokane, have more predictable flat suburban setups.

## Tacoma: South Puget Sound's Family Market

[Tacoma](/locations/washington/tacoma) is the most distinctly working-class major city in Washington — and that's not a slight. Tacoma has genuine neighborhoods with long-established families, real community bonds, and an event culture built on significant occasions. When a family in Tacoma throws a graduation party or anniversary dinner, they mean it.

**The military connection:** Joint Base Lewis-McChord (JBLM) is adjacent to Tacoma and is one of the largest military installations in the Pacific Northwest. Military homecoming events and celebration dinners are a consistent segment here — private hibachi is a compelling format for welcoming someone home.

**Tacoma neighborhoods:**

*North End and Proctor District* — Tacoma's established affluent neighborhoods. Older homes with varied lot sizes. Confirm outdoor square footage.

*South Tacoma and Fircrest* — More recent suburban construction, more consistent outdoor setups.

*Federal Way and Des Moines (along the King/Pierce County line)* — Growing bedroom communities between Tacoma and Seattle with suburban lot profiles and strong family event demand.

*Puyallup and Sumner* — Pierce County suburbs with active graduation and family event markets. Good outdoor setups in newer construction.

**University of Puget Sound:** Graduation in May generates family celebrations throughout North Tacoma.
