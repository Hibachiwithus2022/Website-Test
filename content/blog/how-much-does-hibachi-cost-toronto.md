Before booking a private hibachi chef in Toronto, most people want a straight answer on cost before anything else. Here's the complete breakdown of what a Toronto hibachi event actually costs, in Canadian dollars.

---

## The Base Rate

At Hibachi Connect, Toronto and GTA pricing is straightforward:

- **Adults: $78 CAD per person**
- **Children (ages 4–12): $40 CAD per person**
- **Children 3 and under: free**
- **Event minimum: $780 CAD**

The event minimum means a small gathering is billed at $780 CAD regardless of headcount — once your group's per-person total passes that number, you're simply paying the per-person rate.

---

## What's Included in the Base Rate

- A certified teppanyaki chef and full live cooking performance
- Two protein choices per person (chicken, steak, shrimp, salmon, tofu, and more)
- Hibachi fried rice, grilled vegetables, salad, and miso soup
- House-made yum yum, ginger, and teriyaki sauces
- All plates, chopsticks, and serving equipment
- Full setup before the event and complete cleanup after

There's no separate setup fee and no hidden charge for the equipment — the per-person rate covers the entire experience from arrival to cleanup.

## What Affects Your Final Quote

- **Guest count** — the primary driver of total cost, since pricing is per person
- **Premium protein selections** — filet mignon and lobster tail are the most common upgrades
- **Appetizer add-ons** — gyoza and edamame are available for an additional cost
- **Travel distance** — most [Toronto](/locations/ontario/toronto) and GTA bookings have no additional travel fee; farther outlying areas may have a small travel charge, confirmed at booking
