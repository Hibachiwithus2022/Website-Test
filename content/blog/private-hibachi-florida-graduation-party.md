Florida's graduation season runs from late April through early June and generates more concentrated demand — across restaurants, rental properties, and private event vendors — than almost any other calendar window in the state. The University of Florida's commencement is one of the largest single graduation events in the country. FSU, UCF, USF, UM, and FAU all hold their ceremonies within the same six-week window. And across every Florida metro, the extended family gatherings that define Southern graduation culture mean a party of 18 or 24 guests is standard, not exceptional.

Private hibachi solves the problem that every Florida restaurant in May cannot: a chef arrives at your home or rental property, sets up with the complete self-contained grill, and performs the full teppanyaki dinner for your entire group at once. The graduate, their grandparents, their college roommates, their cousins, and both sets of parents all eat together for 90 minutes without a reservation battle, a two-table compromise, or a wait.

## University of Florida — Gainesville

The University of Florida commencement is the largest single graduation event in Florida by attendance. UF holds multiple ceremonies across two to three days in early May, drawing families from every Florida county and from neighboring Georgia, Alabama, and South Carolina. [Gainesville](/locations/florida/gainesville) restaurant capacity is exhausted during commencement weekend — the town-and-gown mismatch is more severe here than at any other Florida university city.

[Gainesville](/locations/florida/gainesville) families hosting graduation parties at their homes, and families renting houses in the area for commencement weekend, face the same situation: a group of 16 to 24 guests who need a dinner that feels as significant as the occasion. Private hibachi at a Gainesville or Alachua County address handles this directly.

The geographic range extends well beyond Gainesville proper. Families in [Jacksonville](/locations/florida/jacksonville), [Ponte Vedra Beach](/locations/florida/ponte-vedra-beach), [Daytona Beach](/locations/florida/daytona-beach), and [Palm Coast](/locations/florida/palm-coast) who have graduates at UF often host graduation dinners at their home address rather than in Gainesville — the chef travels to wherever the party is.

**Booking window for UF graduation:** The specific Saturday and Sunday after each commencement ceremony are the most competitive dates in the entire state. Book these 6–8 weeks before commencement. For other May weekend dates in the Gainesville corridor, 5–6 weeks ahead is sufficient.

## Florida State University — Tallahassee

FSU holds commencement in [Tallahassee](/locations/florida/tallahassee) in mid-May. The FSU graduation market draws families from across North Florida and the Florida Panhandle — [Pensacola](/locations/florida/pensacola) families, [Panama City Beach](/locations/florida/panama-city-beach) families, and Jacksonville-area families all travel to Tallahassee for FSU commencement or host their graduation dinners at home.

[Tallahassee](/locations/florida/tallahassee) residential events during graduation week follow the same pattern as Gainesville — local families with large backyards or covered outdoor spaces hosting the graduate's full family and friend group.

**Booking window for FSU graduation:** Book 5–7 weeks before commencement. For Tallahassee-area addresses during graduation week, weekend dates fill 5–7 weeks out.

## UCF — Orlando

University of Central Florida commencement is held in [Orlando](/locations/florida/orlando) in early May. UCF is one of the largest universities in the country by enrollment, and its commuter-school model means graduation families span a wide geographic range — [Kissimmee](/locations/florida/kissimmee), [Davenport](/locations/florida/davenport), [Clermont](/locations/florida/clermont), [Winter Garden](/locations/florida/winter-garden), and all of Orange and Seminole counties.

Orlando-area graduation parties follow the same residential format as the rest of Florida: families with homes in the Disney corridor's surrounding communities, in the Lake Nona and Horizon West developments, and across the broader Orange County metro host their graduation dinners at home rather than competing for restaurant reservations.

UCF graduation overlaps with peak Disney-area vacation rental demand, which compresses available chef availability further than a single-university event would alone.

**Booking window for UCF graduation:** Confirm 5–7 weeks before commencement. Orlando-area weekend dates during graduation season fill 5–6 weeks ahead.

## USF — Tampa

University of South Florida commencement is held in [Tampa](/locations/florida/tampa) in early May. [Tampa](/locations/florida/tampa), [Wesley Chapel](/locations/florida/wesley-chapel), [Riverview](/locations/florida/riverview), [Clearwater](/locations/florida/clearwater), [St. Petersburg](/locations/florida/st-petersburg), and [Bradenton](/locations/florida/bradenton) families all host USF graduation parties. The Tampa Bay area outdoor setup is consistently good — screened lanais, pool decks, and covered patios across the metro make the private hibachi format straightforward.

**Booking window for USF graduation:** Confirm 5–6 weeks before commencement.

## University of Miami — Coral Gables

University of Miami commencement is held in mid-May. UM draws nationally — New York, Chicago, and West Coast families travel to South Florida for commencement, and the [Coral Gables](/locations/florida/coral-gables) and [Miami](/locations/florida/miami) estate market captures much of this demand. UM graduation parties at Coral Gables estate properties run 16 to 28 guests and skew heavily toward premium setups.

[Fort Lauderdale](/locations/florida/fort-lauderdale), [Boca Raton](/locations/florida/boca-raton), [Hollywood](/locations/florida/hollywood), and [Aventura](/locations/florida/aventura) families hosting UM graduation dinners at their homes represent the residential version of this market.

UM graduation overlaps with the tail end of South Florida spring break season, which adds demand pressure beyond the graduation market alone.

**Booking window for UM graduation:** Book 5–7 weeks before commencement.

## Florida High School Graduation Season

Florida's major metro areas have high school commencement corridors that run from mid-May through early June:

**South Florida corridor (Miami-Dade and Broward counties):** The largest high school graduation market in the state. Coral Gables High, Belen Jesuit, Christopher Columbus High, and the major Broward County high schools all have commencements in May. South Florida families host graduation dinners in [Coral Gables](/locations/florida/coral-gables), [Aventura](/locations/florida/aventura), [Hollywood](/locations/florida/hollywood), [Boca Raton](/locations/florida/boca-raton), and [Delray Beach](/locations/florida/delray-beach).

**Orlando/Central Florida corridor:** Windermere, Dr. Phillips, Lake Mary, and Seminole County high school commencements run through May and early June. [Winter Garden](/locations/florida/winter-garden), [Clermont](/locations/florida/clermont), and the Four Corners area families host graduation parties throughout this window.

**Tampa Bay corridor:** Plant High, Jesuit High, Carrollwood Day School, and the major Hillsborough and Pinellas County high schools have May commencements. [Tampa](/locations/florida/tampa), [Wesley Chapel](/locations/florida/wesley-chapel), and [St. Petersburg](/locations/florida/st-petersburg) families host graduation dinners throughout this window.

**Northeast Florida (Jacksonville) corridor:** Episcopal, Bolles, and the major Duval and St. Johns County high schools graduate in late May. [Jacksonville](/locations/florida/jacksonville) and [Ponte Vedra Beach](/locations/florida/ponte-vedra-beach) graduation parties run through the end of May.

**Booking window for Florida high school graduation:** Peak graduation Saturdays in the major metro corridors should be confirmed 5–7 weeks ahead.

## Practical Guidance for Any Florida Graduation Party

**Group sizing:** Most Florida graduation parties run 14 to 28 guests. Single-station setup (one chef, one grill) handles up to 25 guests simultaneously. For groups of 26–40, dual-station (two chefs, two grills) serves everyone at once with coordinated performance.

**Menu:** Every guest orders individually. The chef handles multiple protein selections simultaneously — chicken, steak, shrimp, and salmon in any combination. Premium upgrades (filet mignon, lobster tail, wagyu) are available for the graduate or the full group.

**Setup:** A 12×12 outdoor area — screened lanai, pool deck, covered patio, or backyard. Florida residential properties almost universally have suitable outdoor space.

**Timing:** The full dinner runs 90–120 minutes. Plan your graduation evening around this — guests arrive, the chef performs the dinner, and the celebration continues naturally.
